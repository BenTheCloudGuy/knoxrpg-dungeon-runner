# Scrying Stone

Notes: 
- The Crystals are keys to the exit and ways to gain clues to where treasure, puzzels, etc..
- It will also help the players learn more about the Dungeon and Xhal’theris.
- Each Crystal will give a clue to one of the bigger Treasure Items. 
- The first Scrying Stone is located in the same room the players wake up in. 


The Scrying stone -> tied to 8 schools of magic represented by 8 Colored Crystals.
- inserting a crystal brings up 4 options the players can select from.. Each option will give them information about a room, puzzle, or prize in the dungeon. 


## Crystal → School → LED Colour

| Crystal Colour | School | LED Hex |
|---|---|---|
| White  | Abjuration    | `0xFFFFFF` |
| Blue   | Conjuration   | `0x0000FF` |
| Yellow | Divination    | `0xFFFF00` |
| Pink   | Enchantment   | `0xFF0080` |
| Red    | Evocation     | `0xFF0000` |
| Orange | Illusion      | `0xFF8000` |
| Black  | Necromancy    | `0x000000` |
| Green  | Transmutation | `0x00FF00` |
