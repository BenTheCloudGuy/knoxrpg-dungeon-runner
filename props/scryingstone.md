# Scrying Stone

Notes: 
- The Crystals are keys to the exit and to color-locked doors in the dungeon.
- The Scrying Stone reveals clues for crystal color-locked doors and the correct exit-lock order.
- It will also help the players learn more about the Dungeon and Xhal’theris.
- The first Scrying Stone is located in the Portal Room. 


The Scrying stone -> tied to 7 Crystal Shards.
- inserting a crystal brings up 4 options the players can select from. Each option gives them information about a color-locked door, the exit-lock order, a room, or Xhal’theris.


## Crystal → School → LED Colour

| Crystal Colour | School | LED Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Green  | Transmutation | `0x00FF00` |
| White  | Abjuration    | `0xFFFFFF` |
| Yellow | Divination    | `0xFFFF00` |
| Blue   | Conjuration   | `0x0000FF` |
| Purple | Enchantment   | `0x800080` |
| Red    | Evocation     | `0xFF0000` |
| Black  | Necromancy    | `0x000000` |
