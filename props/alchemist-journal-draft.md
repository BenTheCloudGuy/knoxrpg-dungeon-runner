## Journal Page 2

### Potion of Acid Resistance

This one is for the vat work. It gives resistance to acid damage for 1 hour. The shell has to form before the oil goes in. If the oil goes first, it separates and the whole batch is waste.

**Ingredients**

- 2 pinches Powdered Pearl
- 1 spoonful Black Dragon Scale Powder
- 3 drops Wyvern Venom
- 1 spoonful Troll Blood
- 1 pinch Blessed Hart Antler Powder
- 1 measure Moonwater

**Method**

1. Pour the clear, rain-scented Moonwater into the silver cauldron. Shake until the blue-white sparks vanish.
2. Stir in the Powdered Pearl until silver ribbons circle the bottom.
3. Add the Black Dragon Scale Powder with the iron stirring rod.
4. Add the Troll Blood. Keep stirring until the surface stops pitting the rod.
5. Mix in the Wyvern Venom, then dust in the Blessed Hart Antler Powder.
6. Cool in the bowl before bottling.

The successful batch turns pearl-gray. A thin shell climbs the bottle walls. The vinegar smell fades slowly.

Wrong order: the liquid beads into greasy yellow globules, spits against the metal, then cools inert. Sour film on the spoon.

[Correction: Troll Blood before Wyvern Venom. I wrote this twice. It is still true.]

[Production note for Senshi: sketch Powdered Pearl making silver ribbons in Moonwater, and a small iron rod with pits along its tip.]

## Journal Page 3

### Potion of Fire Resistance

Resistance to fire damage for 1 hour. The burner does most of the work. Do not rush the cooling grain.

**Ingredients**

- 1 dram Fire Beetle Gland
- 1 pinch Charcoal of Salamander Bone
- 3 drops Honey of the Sun Bee
- 1 measure Cinder Reed
- 1 sprig Kingsfoil
- 1 pinch Frostsalt

**Method**

1. Warm the Cinder Reed over the burner until it exhales one ember.
2. Add the Fire Beetle Gland while the ember is visible.
3. Crush the Kingsfoil and stir it in with the glass stirring rod.
4. Add the Charcoal of Salamander Bone. The red flecks should brighten near the flame.
5. Drip in the Honey of the Sun Bee only after the mixture stops smoking.
6. Finish with the Frostsalt.

Success: a small sun flares beneath the cauldron, then folds into a calm red glow. The bottle warms the hand without burning it.

Wrong order: white flash, bitter smoke, cold gritty sludge. Cannot bottle.

[Stock note: Fire Beetle Gland is down to one dram. Xhal'theris wants three bottles. He can have one bottle.]

[Production note for Senshi: sketch Fire Beetle Gland, Cinder Reed with its ember, and Frostsalt forming a skin of ice.]

## Journal Page 4

### Potion of Poison Resistance

Resistance to poison damage for 1 hour. The fruit must stain the liquid before Powdered Pearl clears it. The moss catches what the fruit gives off.

**Ingredients**

- 2 pinches Nightshade Berries
- 1 measure Moonwater
- 1 tuft Grave Moss
- 1 sprig Kingsfoil
- 1 pinch Powdered Pearl
- 1 pod Silted Star Anise
- 1 pinch Blessed Hart Antler Powder

**Method**

1. Put the Grave Moss in the cooling bowl. Expose it to the brightest light available. Wait for it to contract.
2. Add the Moonwater after the fibers contract.
3. Crush the Nightshade Berries until the purple stain appears. Stir them in.
4. Add the Kingsfoil and let the surface settle.
5. Dust in the Powdered Pearl.
6. Add the Silted Star Anise. Wait for it to point toward the nearest metal.
7. Add the Blessed Hart Antler Powder last.

Success: purple vapor curls up, the moss catches it, and the batch collapses into a clear green drink with a fresh herbal smell.

Wrong order: purple stain clots into black beads. Wet rot smell. Cloudy after cooling.

[Complaint: The brightest light available is a cracked lamp. Whoever designed this place did not brew this batch.]

[Production note for Senshi: sketch contracted Grave Moss in the cooling bowl and Silted Star Anise pointing at the iron rod.]

## Journal Page 5

### Lesser Healing Potion

Restores 2d4 + 2 hit points. A gentle repair draught if the glassroot goes in after the syrup. Before it, the drink sets hard.

**Ingredients**

- 1 spoonful Troll Blood
- 3 drops Honey of the Sun Bee
- 1 measure Moonwater
- 1 sprig Kingsfoil
- 1 pinch Blessed Hart Antler Powder
- 1 tuft Grave Moss
- 1 pinch Glassroot Sap

**Method**

1. Warm the Moonwater until its sparks rise to the surface.
2. Stir in the Troll Blood until every groove it makes closes.
3. Add the Grave Moss and wait for it to contract.
4. Crush the Kingsfoil into the cauldron.
5. Add the Honey of the Sun Bee in three slow drops.
6. Stretch a thread of Glassroot Sap over the surface and stir it through.
7. Dust in the Blessed Hart Antler Powder. Bottle while warm.

Success: the red liquid pulses like a heartbeat. Fine glassy threads knit over the surface. Warm honey and mint.

Wrong order: red clots and clear water. The clots seal around the spoon. Nothing heals. It will not pour.

[Margin: Keep one dose for the hand. This is a sensible use of stock, unlike most orders from above.]

[Production note for Senshi: sketch Glassroot Sap stretched between two spoons, hardening into a glass filament.]

## Journal Page 6

### Greater Healing Potion

Restores 4d4 + 4 hit points. Stronger draught. The Nightshade Berries are the bitter catalyst. They must dissolve before Troll Blood.

**Ingredients**

- 2 spoonfuls Troll Blood
- 1 dram Fire Beetle Gland
- 3 drops Honey of the Sun Bee
- 1 measure Moonwater
- 1 pinch Blessed Hart Antler Powder
- 1 pinch Nightshade Berries
- 1 sprig Kingsfoil

**Method**

1. Crush the Nightshade Berries in the mortar until the purple stain is even.
2. Pour in the Moonwater. Stir until its sparks move in a single ring.
3. Add the Fire Beetle Gland and wait for the ring to brighten.
4. Fold in the Troll Blood in two portions.
5. Add the Kingsfoil.
6. Drip in the Honey of the Sun Bee only when the brew smells clean and sharp.
7. Dust in the Blessed Hart Antler Powder.

Success: two red rings race around the cauldron, then sink into a luminous ruby drink. Hot honey, mint, clean stone.

Wrong order: violet crust on top. The crystals go dark. It looks drinkable and has no restorative warmth.

[Correction: 2 spoonfuls Troll Blood. Not 1. The first line was hidden under a stain.]

## Journal Page 7

### Jumping Potion

For 1 hour, long jump and high jump distances triple, with advantage on Athletics checks made to jump. Lift without a broken ankle, if the dose is right.

**Ingredients**

- 3 drops Honey of the Sun Bee
- 1 measure Moonwater
- 2 pinches Powdered Pearl
- 1 pinch Blessed Hart Antler Powder
- 1 sprig Kingsfoil
- 1 pinch Thunderhead Lichen
- 1 seed Echo Seed

**Method**

1. Shake the Moonwater until blue-white sparks fill it.
2. Tap the Thunderhead Lichen with the glass rod. Catch its tiny spark in the liquid.
3. Add the Powdered Pearl. Stir until silver ribbons rise.
4. Crush in the Kingsfoil.
5. Add the Honey of the Sun Bee in three drops.
6. Roll the Echo Seed around the cauldron once. Let it repeat the last tap.
7. Finish with the Blessed Hart Antler Powder.

The successful potion leaps from the cauldron in a clean arc and lands without splashing. Every bubble hangs for a heartbeat before rising.

Wrong order: flat and heavy. One bubble drops to the bottom. It tastes like cold syrup. The lift is gone.

[Marginal note: The Echo Seed repeats the last tap. This is useful. It is also irritating.]

[Production note for Senshi: sketch Thunderhead Lichen sparking under the glass rod and Echo Seed rolling around the cauldron.]

## Journal Page 8

### Potion of Climbing

Gives a climb speed equal to the drinker's speed for 1 hour, with advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks made to climb. The thread must pull taut before the syrup.

**Ingredients**

- 1 spoonful Grave Moss
- 2 sprigs Kingsfoil
- 1 measure Moonwater
- 1 pinch Blessed Hart Antler Powder
- 1 pinch Ironvine Thread
- 3 drops Honey of the Sun Bee

**Method**

1. Soak the Grave Moss in Moonwater until the fibers contract.
2. Add the Kingsfoil and crush it against the cauldron wall.
3. Lower the Ironvine Thread into the mixture. Stir until it pulls taut.
4. Dust in the Blessed Hart Antler Powder.
5. Add the Honey of the Sun Bee in three drops while the thread remains taut.
6. Remove the thread before bottling.

Success: the potion crawls up the bottle instead of settling. When the stopper comes out, the surface clings in a thick green ribbon.

Wrong order: the liquid sinks like mud. Ironvine Thread snaps into loose fibers. No tack on glass or stone.

[Stock worry: Ironvine Thread is short. Do not spend a length testing the door again.]

[Production note for Senshi: sketch Ironvine Thread pulling taut against the iron stirring rod, with a green ribbon climbing a bottle.]

## Journal Page 9

### Potion of Invisibility

Invisible for 1 hour. The effect ends early if the drinker attacks, deals damage, or casts a spell. Keep the cover on. People always lift the cover.

**Ingredients**

- 2 pinches Powdered Pearl
- 1 measure Moonwater
- 1 pinch Nightshade Berries
- 1 tuft Grave Moss
- 1 pinch Blessed Hart Antler Powder
- 1 pinch Bluecap Spores

**Method**

1. Expose the Grave Moss to bright light until it contracts.
2. Pour Moonwater over it. Keep the cauldron covered.
3. Crush the Nightshade Berries beneath the cover.
4. Add the Bluecap Spores. Breathe across the surface until a blue ring forms.
5. Stir in the Powdered Pearl without lifting the cover.
6. Add the Blessed Hart Antler Powder last.

Success: the cauldron rim disappears first. Then the potion becomes a hole in the light. Sound continues. The liquid cannot be seen moving.

Wrong order: blue ring hangs in the air and pops. The potion settles as a dull gray film that reflects every face around it.

[Complaint: Bluecap Spores in the eyes are difficult to wash out. Use the cover. This has been written before.]

[Production note for Senshi: sketch the Bluecap Spores making a blue ring beneath a cloth cover, with the cauldron rim fading.]

**Ingredients**

- 1 dram Fire Beetle Gland

- 1 measure Moonwater
- 1 pinch Blessed Hart Antler Powder

For 1 minute, this gives a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls. Weapon strikes land with brutal certainty. The static has to be captured before the oil.

**Ingredients**

- 1 dram Fire Beetle Gland
- 1 pinch Charcoal of Salamander Bone
- 2 drops Wyvern Venom

- 1 measure Moonwater
- 3 drops Honey of the Sun Bee
- 1 sprig Kingsfoil

5. Add the Wyvern Venom in two drops.

Success: a sharp crack runs through the bottle. The liquid flashes bronze. Every nearby metal tool points toward it for a moment.

Wrong order: gray foam and scorched-meat smell. The sparks ground out against the cauldron. Weak, oily potion.

[Correction: Wyvern Venom is 2 drops. The first test used 3. It was still poison, only less useful for fighting.]

## Journal Page 12

### The Vat Accident, first account

I was making the Potion of Invulnerability. The batch was correct. I checked the order twice.

The Green Transmutation Crystal was on the bench beside the cauldron. It should have been sealed. I reached for the iron rod. My sleeve caught the crystal, or the rod did. It struck the rim and dropped into the vat.

The vat changed sound first. The surface pulled down around the crystal. Then it rose.

The caustic liquid came over the near edge and burned the skin off my left hand. I saw the fingers. I saw the bone at the first knuckle. The pain arrived after that.

I used the Lesser Healing Potion. One spoonful Troll Blood, 3 drops Honey of the Sun Bee, 1 measure Moonwater, 1 sprig Kingsfoil, 1 pinch Blessed Hart Antler Powder, 1 tuft Grave Moss, 1 pinch Glassroot Sap. It saved the hand. It did not restore it cleanly. The skin is gray at the tips. The fingers are stiff.

[Margin: Keep healing potion beside the burner. This should have been obvious.]

[Production note for Senshi: sketch the Green Transmutation Crystal hitting the vat rim, with one scorched glove or sleeve beside the cauldron.]

## Journal Page 13

### The Vat Accident, second account

The Green Transmutation Crystal is still in the vat. I can see it when the surface clears. The vat closes over it when it does not.

The Potion of Invulnerability can let me recover it. It gives resistance to all damage for exactly about 1 minute. That is the duration. About 1 minute. The hand must be quick.

Drink. Tie the rope. Open the lid. Reach in. Take the crystal. Withdraw. Do not inspect it in the vat. Do not drop it. Do not wait for the shell to harden again.

I can make the potion. The recipe is reliable when the Ironvine Thread is taut before the Troll Blood. I need the Black Dragon Scale Powder to hold the shell, Fire Beetle Gland for the living heat, and Powdered Pearl for the silver suspension. The Blessed Hart Antler Powder clears the final batch.

The vat is louder now. It reacts when I stand near it. I think the crystal is changing the work below the surface. I need to move fast.

[Stock note: one full dose if the bench stock is honest. If the Goblin Trader has Hart Antler Powder, trade for it.]

[Trade note: The Goblin stall at the Drift Stones saves a day's walk. The Drift Stones are still random and dangerous. Ground level has Lava Elementals. I take the bad odds I can measure.]
