# The Void

**Room Type:** Wild Magic Deathtrap / Traversal Hazard
**Display:** 15" monitor — small visible area, limited platforms on screen at any time
**Primary Threat:** The Weave itself. Wild magic is the room.
**Falls:** Lethal. Permadeath dungeon — players know.
**Core Choice:** Cross fast using magic (risky, powerful) or slow without it (safer, slower, still deadly).

## What This Room Is

A failed extradimensional spell tore a hole in reality. The Weave is hemorrhaging here. Floating stones drift in an endless black gulf — they exist only because the chaotic magic is holding them in place. Step wrong, cast wrong, breathe wrong, and the Weave answers.

The wild magic is not a hazard layered on top of a jump puzzle. **It is the room.** Everything else is window dressing.

## Room Description (Read Aloud)

> Your light dies three feet from your torch. Beyond that, black — total, devouring, infinite. Broken stone platforms drift through the dark at impossible angles, some tilted, some spinning slowly, some flickering in and out of sight like a guttering candle.
> The air tastes like copper and ozone. Your hair stands on end. Somewhere in the dark, something that is not a voice is whispering in a language your teeth understand but your ears cannot.

## The Two Paths

When the party enters, they can see (faintly) two routes across:

- **The Lit Path:** Stable, slow, requires no magic. ~8 platforms. Slow crossing means more Weave Pulses hit them.
- **The Bright Path:** Faster, ~4 platforms, but every platform is humming with raw Weave energy. Stepping onto one without magical protection forces a save. Using magic here is *strong* but every spell triggers a surge.

Smart parties split up. That is fine. That is the point.

## The Round (Core Mechanics)

The Void doesn't wait. Every round, two automatic events fire before any player turn — first the platforms shift, then the Weave pulses.

### 1. Platform Drift (Initiative 25)

At the very top of the round, every visible floating platform moves.

- **Default drift:** Each platform has an assigned drift direction (N, S, E, W, NE, NW, SE, SW) set at the start of the encounter. Mark these on the GM map. At the top of each round, every platform drifts 5 ft in its direction.
- **Chaotic platforms:** Mark 1-2 platforms as chaotic. Roll a d8 each round to determine their direction.
- **Collisions:** If two platforms collide, every creature on either makes a DC 12 Dex save or falls prone. Platforms stop where they meet (no overlap).
- **Off-screen drift:** A platform that drifts past the visible monitor area is now in the dark. Anyone on it is isolated and can only shout for help until their platform drifts back into view.
- **Broken jumps:** If two platforms separate beyond 15 ft, the gap is too wide to jump until they drift back.
- **Players can anchor a platform.** As an action, a character grips and braces — DC 15 Athletics or Arcana. On success, that platform does not drift this round.

### 2. Weave Pulse (Initiative 20)

After the platforms settle, the Weave shifts. Roll a d6 on the **Weave Pulse Table**. The new state replaces the previous one and lasts the full round. Give the party a 1-sentence warning before rolling — they feel it coming.

This happens **every round, automatically, no matter what the players do.** They cannot avoid the Pulse. They can only react.

#### Weave Pulse Table (d6)

1. **Gravity Inverts.** Every creature must make a DC 14 Dex save or be flung 20 ft straight up. Fall damage on landing. If no platform is above them, they land back on their own — prone.
2. **Time Skips.** Half the party (DM picks the slowest-acting half) loses their turn this round. The Weave ate it.
3. **The Stones Forget.** A random platform phases out of existence for the round. Anyone standing on it falls. DC 16 Dex save to leap to an adjacent platform as it vanishes. Roll 1d8 to learn which platform.
4. **Magic Floods.** Every creature takes 2d8 force damage (no save). Spells cast this round deal +1d8 damage but automatically trigger a Wild Surge regardless of level.
5. **The Whisper.** Every creature makes a DC 15 Wisdom save or uses their next action attacking the nearest creature (ally or enemy). The Void wants company.
6. **Stillness.** The Weave pauses. Nothing bad happens this round. The room goes silent. The players will not trust it. Good.

**Escalation:** Each time the party reaches a checkpoint platform (every 3rd platform), the GM rolls 2d6 on the Pulse table and applies both effects simultaneously for that round.

## Wild Magic — Casting Spells

Any time a creature casts a spell of any level in this room:

1. Roll a d20.
2. On a **1-10**, roll on the **Wild Surge Table** below.
3. On an **11-20**, the spell works normally — but the caster takes `spell level × 1d6` psychic damage as the Weave bites back.

**There is no safe casting in this room.** Cantrips included.

**Concentration:** Any creature concentrating on a spell in this room makes a DC 15 Con save at the start of each of their turns. Failure ends concentration AND triggers a Wild Surge.

**Healing magic is broken.** Any healing spell has a 50% chance (roll before casting) to instead deal that amount as necrotic damage. The Weave does not want them to live.

### Wild Surge Table (d12)

These effects are deliberately nasty. The Void wants them dead.

1. **Misfire.** The spell targets the caster instead.
2. **Backlash.** Caster takes `2d10 + spell level d10` force damage. No save.
3. **Anchor Lost.** Caster is hurled to a random platform (1d6 platforms away in a random direction). They make a DC 15 Dex save on arrival or fall.
4. **Spell Echo.** The spell goes off three more times this round, at random valid targets (including allies). Caster chooses targets only on the first cast.
5. **Wild Polymorph.** Caster becomes a small harmless animal (sheep, frog, chicken) for 1d4 rounds. Cannot cast. Speed 10. Yes, they can fall.
6. **Soul Tear.** Caster takes 3d10 psychic damage and is stunned until end of next turn.
7. **Rift Opens.** A 5 ft wide rift opens under the caster's platform. The platform splits. Both halves drift apart 10 ft over 2 rounds. Anyone on it must choose a side.
8. **Aging.** Caster ages 1d10 years. Mostly cosmetic, but if it brings them past a meaningful threshold (60+, 80+), apply 1 level of exhaustion.
9. **Spell Theft.** The caster loses one prepared spell or known spell at random. Gone for the rest of the dungeon.
10. **The Void Notices You.** From this point on, every Weave Pulse targets this character first/most harshly. Permanent for the dungeon.
11. **Detonation.** A 20 ft radius force explosion centered on the caster. 4d8 force damage, DC 15 Dex save for half. Caster auto-fails.
12. **Reality Reset.** All buffs, debuffs, summons, conjurations, and ongoing spell effects on every creature in the room end immediately. Including helpful ones the party was counting on.

## Wild Magic Traps & Events

Beyond the round-by-round chaos, specific platforms in the Void have permanent wild magic features. Place 4-6 of these across the visible area at session start — some visible, some hidden until triggered. The Mana Geyser and Echo Platform also fire at the top of each round, between Platform Drift and Weave Pulse.

### Surge Sigil

A platform marked with a faintly glowing rune. The first creature to step on it triggers an immediate **Wild Surge** roll (use the table above). The sigil burns out after one use. Perception DC 14 to spot before stepping.

### Mana Geyser

A platform with a fissure of raw Weave erupting from its center. Anyone standing on it at the top of the round (after Platform Drift, before Weave Pulse) takes 2d6 force damage and rolls a Wild Surge. Moving through it on a turn (not ending there) is safe.

### Resonance Well

A platform crackling with arcane energy that amplifies spells. Any spell cast *from* this platform deals +1d6 damage of its type (or +1d6 healing for healing spells) — but the caster rolls **twice** on the Wild Surge table and the GM takes the worse result. Pure gamble.

### Echo Platform

A platform that records the last spell to touch it. At the top of each round (after Platform Drift, before Weave Pulse) the echo releases that spell again at a random valid target within range. Cantrips count. Effects can stack absurdly if multiple spells are echoed across rounds.

### Phantom Platform

A platform that flickers in and out of reality. On odd rounds it is solid. On even rounds it is illusion — anyone standing on it falls. *Detect Magic* shows it wavering. Investigation DC 15 to notice the pattern after one round of observation.

### Stasis Crack

A platform with a fissure of dead Weave running through it. Any spell cast *on or from* this platform simply fails — no surge, no effect, the slot is still spent. A safe haven for casters who specifically want to NOT cast. Concentration checks made on this platform have advantage.

### Surge Sink (Optional, Strong)

A platform that absorbs nearby instability. If a Wild Surge would trigger on it or an adjacent platform, the surge is canceled and the Sink glows briefly. Recharges after 1 round of cooldown. Place at most one of these in the room — it is a huge gift.

## Falling

This is a permadeath dungeon. Players know. Falls are real.

- A character who falls off a platform is **dead** unless:
  - An ally within 30 ft uses a reaction to attempt a grab (DC 18 Dex or Athletics).
  - The falling character has a way to fly, levitate, or teleport — but casting in this room triggers the Wild Surge table, so this is itself a gamble.
  - The Weave Pulse for the round is **Gravity Inverts**, in which case they fall *up* and land hard but live (4d6 damage).

That's it. There are no soft landings. Players were warned.

## Non-Caster Threats (Real Risk for Everyone)

Wild magic should not only punish casters. Make sure these things hit fighters and rogues too:

- The Weave Pulse hits everyone regardless of class.
- "The Whisper" makes martials attack each other.
- "Magic Floods" deals damage to everyone, full stop.
- Platforms phase out from under heavy-armor characters with poor Dex saves.
- The Bright Path requires either a magical solution or eating the surge save on every platform (DC 14 Con or take 2d6 force damage per platform crossed).


## GM Notes

- This room should kill someone. Probably more than one. The dungeon promised it would.
- Do not soften the surge table. The players chose to cast.
- The "Stillness" result on the Pulse table is critical — without it, the room feels like pure noise. The quiet round is what makes the loud rounds terrifying.
- If the party is being too cautious and stalling, the Weave Pulse handles that for you. They cannot wait it out.
- If a player tries to "out-clever" the wild magic with creative spellwork (Counterspell, Dispel Magic, Antimagic Field), let them try — then roll on the Surge table for the attempt. Antimagic Field in this room triggers a guaranteed result 11 (Detonation).
- The Void wants them. Roleplay it that way. Xhal'theris should narrate from off-screen, delighted.
