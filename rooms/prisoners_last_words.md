# The Prisoner's Last Words

**Room Type:** Story / Loot / Hazard
**Reward:** **The Prisoner's Letter** (critical exposition: the 7-crystal exit), a hand-drawn **Dungeon Map** *(handout TBD when final layout is set)*, healing potions, scroll, daggers, holy symbol, small coin, and **a clue to opening the Artificer's Cube**
**Primary Threat:** A still-armed cage trap that has already claimed one runner. The body is 25 ft overhead. Reaching it is the encounter.
**Solution:** Climb to the cage *without* triggering the floor plates that hauled the previous runner up to die, or trigger the trap *deliberately* and have someone ready to break it open before the next victim suffocates

![Prisoner's Last Words](../images/rooms/prisoners_last_words.png)

## Placement Note

This room is **placed just before the mid-game shift** — i.e., somewhere between the opening cells/early caverns and the deeper crystal rooms. Its narrative job is to *land the seven-crystal exit reveal* on the party at the moment they are starting to ask "why are we collecting these?" Drop it in the rotation accordingly.

## Room Description

A small, square stone chamber — maybe 25 ft × 25 ft — at the end of a side passage. The ceiling is unusually high (about 30 ft). The floor is dusted with old grit and bone-meal, and in the center, suspended from a heavy iron chain bolted into the ceiling, hangs an **iron cage.**

Inside the cage: a **slumped human skeleton**, still in tattered leather armor, one bony hand clenched white-knuckle around a folded square of parchment. A satchel hangs from his belt. A short sword rests against the cage bars at his hip.

The cage swings, very slightly, in air that should not be moving.

**Read Aloud (on entry):**

> The passage opens into a small stone chamber. The ceiling vaults higher than it should — thirty feet or more — and in the dead center of that ceiling, a heavy iron chain descends to a single iron cage suspended in the air, about twenty-five feet above the floor.
> Inside the cage is a man. Or what used to be a man. He's been dead a long time — decades, by the look of him. Tattered leathers, a satchel at his belt, a short sword resting against the bars. One of his hands is clenched around a folded square of parchment.
> The cage turns slowly in the still air. The chain creaks. The floor below it is scuffed with old bone-dust and faint, faded scratch marks, as if something heavy was dragged across it a very long time ago.
> *He was here for a while. He kept moving until he stopped.*

## The Trap (The Cage)

The mechanism is decades old but still functional. The previous runner triggered it; the trap **reset itself** when the chain went slack again (his body suspended overhead).

- **Trigger:** Stepping on any of the four hidden pressure plates in the center 10×10 ft of the chamber. **Passive Perception 16** to spot the plates (subtle floor wear from the original trap-spring). **DC 15 Investigation** to map them precisely. **DC 16 Thieves' Tools** to disable a plate (per plate, ~1 minute per attempt).
- **Effect when triggered:**
  1. A second cage drops from a concealed ceiling cavity directly above the triggering plate. **DC 15 Dex save.**
  2. **On a fail:** The PC is **caught inside the cage**, lifted to 20 ft in one round by a spring mechanism, and locked. The cage is then *suspended* alongside the existing one until someone breaks them out.
  3. **On a success:** The PC dives clear. The empty cage rises to 20 ft and hangs there. The pressure plate disarms (one-shot per plate).
- **Inside the cage:** The captive is **restrained**, cannot move, and begins **suffocating** if not freed within 5 minutes (the original cage is airtight when latched — that's how the previous runner died). Track this in real time at the table.
- **Breaking the cage:** AC 17, HP 40, immune to poison/psychic. Damage from inside or outside both work. A successful **DC 18 Thieves' Tools** check on the lock (action) opens it cleanly.
- **Once a PC is caged:** They drop on a chain to a controlled descent if the lock is picked. If the cage is destroyed, they fall 20 ft — **2d6 bludgeoning, prone.**

**Player takeaway:** The trap is *survivable* but punishing. A party that just walks in unscrutinizing loses a PC for at least one round and risks suffocating them. A party that scouts the floor first finds the safe perimeter.

## Reaching the Body

Once the floor is understood, the cage is **25 ft up and a 10-ft horizontal distance from the nearest wall.** Options:

| Method | Check / Cost | Outcome |
|--------|--------------|---------|
| **Climb the chain** | DC 15 Athletics, full action per 10 ft of climb | Slow but safe. Two failures = fall (1d6 per 10 ft). |
| **Climb the wall + jump to the cage** | DC 13 Athletics to wall + DC 14 Acrobatics for the leap | Faster. Fail the jump = fall (2d6, prone). |
| **Spider Climb / Levitate / Fly / Misty Step / etc.** | Spell slot or class feature | Trivializes the access — let them have it. |
| **Lasso & winch the cage down** | DC 14 Dex (rope use) + DC 15 Str (collective hauling, up to 4 PCs assist) | Brings the cage to floor level. Takes 3 rounds. **WARNING:** While the cage is descending, the floor plates are still live. |
| **Knock the chain out / shoot the anchor** | Damage the anchor or the chain to drop the cage | Cage falls 25 ft. Body, satchel, and sword all take **3d6 bludgeoning** — destroys 1 healing potion automatically, 50% chance per other fragile item. Letter and map survive (parchment, leather-folded). |

**Letter retrieval:** The skeleton's bony hand has clenched the parchment for decades. Pry it free with a **DC 12 Strength** check or **snap the finger bones** (no check, same result, slightly grim).

## The Clue to the Artificer's Cube

Written in the letter itself, near the end, almost as a footnote:

> *"The cube would not open for me. It wanted three turns, not four. Green to gold, gold to red, red to green. I could not finish the fourth. May you have the wits I did not."*

This is the **opening sequence for the Artificer's Cube** (see `props/ArtificersCube.md`). Decode hint for the GM: the cube's color faces must be aligned in the rotation **Green → Gold → Red → Green** to release the Purple Crystal inside. The "fourth" the prisoner failed to find is the *final* color-lock — but the prisoner gave the party the first three rotations, which is most of the puzzle.

*(If the Artificer's Cube mechanics get redesigned, update this hint to match.)*

## GM Notes

- **Pacing:** This is a *quiet* room. After a string of combat rooms (Cells, Mimic Lake, Goblins' Lair), let the table breathe here. The horror is in the slow swing of the cage and the realization that *someone else came this way and died alone.*
- **Hand the letter out at the table, physically.** Print it on aged paper if you have it. Let one player read it aloud to the group. This is where the **"seven crystals to escape"** reveal lands. Watch faces.
- **Do not pre-warn about the trap.** Let one player walk in unsuspecting. The trap should *happen* to the party, not be theorized about beforehand. If they pass perception, fine — they get to scout. If not, they get to *experience* what the previous runner experienced.
- **The suffocation timer is real.** If a PC is caged and the party panics, *let them* panic. Track minutes on a physical card or timer. This is the room's only real teeth, and it should bite.
- **Don't let them skip the loot.** If the party decides "ugh, too much effort, let's leave," gently remind them: *the letter is up there.* They will need what's in the letter. The room is the gate to the next narrative beat.
- **Map handout:** Once your final dungeon layout is locked, draw the prisoner's hand-drawn map deliberately *imperfect* — wrong rooms, missing exits, wishful thinking. It should be **mostly useful**, not authoritative. Players who treat it as gospel should suffer a little.

## Xhal'theris Flavor

Lines to drop:

- **On entry:** *"Oh. Him. Yes. He lasted longer than most. I was beginning to like him. He died of his own caution, in the end — too slow to commit, too proud to ask his cage for mercy. A pity. A small pity."*
- **When a PC triggers the floor plate:** *"Ah. The same step. The very same step. Some of you walk in the footprints of the dead and don't even notice. How lovely."*
- **When a PC is caged and the suffocation starts:** *"The cage is airtight, you know. He learned that. He had a great deal of time to learn it. I do hope your friends are faster than his were."*
- **When the letter is read aloud:** *"Ahh. He kept writing, right to the end. Touching. A man's last thoughts always taste so much sharper than his first. Read it aloud, little morsel. Read it all."*
- **When the party realizes they need seven crystals:** *"Oh! He told you, did he? Good of him. Yes. *Seven.* You have… how many, now? I lose count when you do."*
- **When the party leaves:** *"Take his things. He won't mind. He waited a long time for you. The least you can do is *finish what he started.*"*

## Adjacent Areas

- **Approach passage:** A short side-tunnel branching from one of the main cavern arteries. Easy to miss if the party rushes. Reward perception or curiosity to find it.
- **Continuation exit:** The room is a *dead end* in terms of dungeon progression — the party comes in, gets the letter, and leaves the way they came. This is intentional: the room is *narrative cargo,* not a path-forward gate.
