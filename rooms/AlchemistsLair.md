# Alchemist's Workshop

![1789589025686](image/AlchemistsLair/1789589025686.png)


There are **20 unlabeled ingredients** on the bench. Ten potion recipes can be produced from them. Several ingredients deliberately appear in multiple recipes, so players cannot solve the puzzle by simple elimination. The players must read the recipe book and use the descriptions and clues to create the potion of Invulnerability so they can survive going into the large chemical vat in the center of the room and retrieve the treasure there (Green Crystal and gold in a small chest).

The room contains a mortar and pestle, brass measuring spoon, glass stirring rod, iron stirring rod, burner, cooling bowl, ten empty potion bottles, and a small silver cauldron. Not all ingredients need to be available in sufficient quantity to create every recipe. The DM should place only the quantities intended for this run.

## Potion Recipes

# Potion of Acid Resistance

**Outcome**: When you drink this potion, you gain resistance to acid damage for 1 hour.

### Ingredients

- 2 pinches Powdered Pearl
- 1 spoonful Black Dragon Scale Powder
- 3 drops Wyvern Venom
- 1 spoonful Troll Blood
- 1 pinch Blessed Hart Antler Powder
- 1 measure Moonwater

**Rationale**: Silver suspension, mineral cleansing, and a self-sealing binder make a coating that refuses corrosive liquid.

### Brewing Instructions

1. Pour the rain-scented clear liquid into the silver cauldron and shake the vessel until blue-white sparks vanish.
2. Stir in the impossibly smooth ivory powder until silver ribbons circle the bottom.
3. Add the vinegar-biting black-green flakes with the iron stirring rod.
4. Add the self-closing copper-smelling paste and keep stirring until the surface stops pitting the rod.
5. Mix in the clear yellow oil, then dust in the white crystals that clarify dirty water.
6. Cool in the bowl before bottling. The self-closing paste must be added before the oil or the oil separates.

### Successful Reaction

> The brew turns pearl-gray. A thin shell climbs the bottle walls, and the smell of vinegar fades slowly.

### Wrong-Order Clue

> The liquid beads into greasy yellow globules, then spits against the metal. It cools inert and leaves a sour film on the spoon.

# Potion of Fire Resistance

**Outcome**: When you drink this potion, you gain resistance to fire damage for 1 hour.

### Ingredients

- 1 dram Fire Beetle Gland
- 1 pinch Charcoal of Salamander Bone
- 3 drops Honey of the Sun Bee
- 1 measure Cinder Reed
- 1 sprig Kingsfoil
- 1 pinch Frostsalt

**Rationale**: Living ember, controlled flame, and a cooling counterweight turn heat into a harmless hearth-glow.

### Brewing Instructions

1. Warm the clear reed over the burner until it exhales one ember.
2. Add the orange-red crystals while the ember is visible.
3. Crush the silver-backed green leaves and stir them in with the glass rod.
4. Add the black powder whose red flecks brighten near flame.
5. Drip in the amber syrup only after the mixture stops smoking.
6. Finish with the blue grains that freeze warm water. If they enter before the syrup, the brew quenches dead.

### Successful Reaction

> A small sun flares beneath the cauldron, then folds into a calm red glow. The finished potion warms the hand without burning it.

### Wrong-Order Clue

> The brew flashes white, coughs out bitter smoke, and leaves a cold, gritty sludge that cannot be bottled.

# Potion of Poison Resistance

**Outcome**: When you drink this potion, you gain resistance to poison damage for 1 hour.

### Ingredients

- 2 pinches Nightshade Berries
- 1 measure Moonwater
- 1 tuft Grave Moss
- 1 sprig Kingsfoil
- 1 pinch Powdered Pearl
- 1 pod Silted Star Anise
- 1 pinch Blessed Hart Antler Powder

**Rationale**: Bitter toxins are drawn into a damp, living filter and locked in a clean mineral suspension.

### Brewing Instructions

1. Place the gray-green fibers in the cooling bowl and expose them to the brightest light available.
2. Add the clear rain-scented liquid after the fibers contract.
3. Crush the violet-black fruit until the purple stain appears, then stir it in.
4. Add the pine-and-mint leaves and let the surface settle.
5. Dust in the smooth ivory powder.
6. Add the rust-colored star pod and wait for it to point toward the nearest metal.
7. Add the clarifying white crystals last. The fruit must stain the liquid before the mineral powder clears it.

### Successful Reaction

> Purple vapor curls from the cauldron, is caught by the moss, and collapses into a clear green drink with a fresh herbal smell.

### Wrong-Order Clue

> The purple stain clots into black beads. The mixture smells of wet rot and remains cloudy after cooling.

# Lesser Healing Potion

**Outcome**: When you drink this potion, you regain 2d4 + 2 hit points.

### Ingredients

- 1 spoonful Troll Blood
- 3 drops Honey of the Sun Bee
- 1 measure Moonwater
- 1 sprig Kingsfoil
- 1 pinch Blessed Hart Antler Powder
- 1 tuft Grave Moss
- 1 pinch Glassroot Sap

**Rationale**: A self-sealing red binder, clean water, and flexible glassroot make a gentle repair draught.

### Brewing Instructions

1. Warm the clear rain-scented liquid until its sparks rise to the surface.
2. Stir in the copper-smelling paste until every groove it makes closes.
3. Add the gray-green fibers and wait for them to contract.
4. Crush the pine-and-mint leaves into the cauldron.
5. Add the amber syrup in three slow drops.
6. Stretch a thread of clear sap over the surface and stir it through. The sap must enter after the syrup or the drink sets hard.
7. Dust in the clarifying white crystals and bottle while warm.

### Successful Reaction

> The red liquid pulses like a heartbeat. Fine glassy threads knit across the surface, and the aroma becomes warm honey and mint.

### Wrong-Order Clue

> The mixture separates into red clots and clear water. The clots seal around the spoon, but nothing heals and the potion will not pour.

# Greater Healing Potion

**Outcome**: When you drink this potion, you regain 4d4 + 4 hit points.

### Ingredients

- 2 spoonfuls Troll Blood
- 1 dram Fire Beetle Gland
- 3 drops Honey of the Sun Bee
- 1 measure Moonwater
- 1 pinch Blessed Hart Antler Powder
- 1 pinch Nightshade Berries
- 1 sprig Kingsfoil

**Rationale**: The stronger draught uses controlled heat and a carefully dosed bitter catalyst to force rapid restoration.

### Brewing Instructions

1. Crush the violet-black fruit in the mortar until the purple stain is even.
2. Pour in the clear rain-scented liquid and stir until its sparks move in a single ring.
3. Add the warm orange-red crystals and wait for that ring to brighten.
4. Fold in the copper-smelling paste in two portions.
5. Add the silver-backed leaves.
6. Drip in the amber syrup only when the brew smells clean and sharp.
7. Dust in the white clarifying crystals. The bitter catalyst must be dissolved before the self-sealing paste or the mixture turns inert.

### Successful Reaction

> Two red rings race around the cauldron, then sink into a luminous ruby drink. It smells of hot honey, mint, and clean stone.

### Wrong-Order Clue

> A violet crust forms over the top and the crystals go dark. The potion remains drinkable-looking but has no restorative warmth.

# Jumping Potion

**Outcome**: For 1 hour, your long jump and high jump distances triple, and you have advantage on Athletics checks made to jump.

### Ingredients

- 3 drops Honey of the Sun Bee
- 1 measure Moonwater
- 2 pinches Powdered Pearl
- 1 pinch Blessed Hart Antler Powder
- 1 sprig Kingsfoil
- 1 pinch Thunderhead Lichen
- 1 seed Echo Seed

**Rationale**: Spark, lift, and buoyant sweetness create a drink that makes the body feel briefly weightless.

### Brewing Instructions

1. Shake the clear rain-scented liquid until blue-white sparks fill it.
2. Tap the yellow lichen with the glass rod and catch its tiny spark in the liquid.
3. Add the smooth ivory powder and stir until silver ribbons rise.
4. Crush in the silver-backed leaves.
5. Add the amber syrup in three drops.
6. Roll the pale clicking seed around the cauldron once, letting it repeat the last tap.
7. Finish with the white crystals that clear dirty water. The lichen spark must be caught before the syrup or the lift collapses.

### Successful Reaction

> The potion leaps from the cauldron in a perfect arc and lands back without splashing. Every bubble hangs for a heartbeat before rising.

### Wrong-Order Clue

> The liquid goes flat and heavy. A single bubble drops to the bottom instead of rising, and the brew tastes like cold syrup.

# Potion of Climbing

**Outcome**: When you drink this potion, you gain a climb speed equal to your speed for 1 hour, and you have advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks made to climb.

### Ingredients

- 1 spoonful Grave Moss
- 2 sprigs Kingsfoil
- 1 measure Moonwater
- 1 pinch Blessed Hart Antler Powder
- 1 pinch Ironvine Thread
- 3 drops Honey of the Sun Bee

**Rationale**: Damp growth, gripping fibers, and a mineral finish make the drink cling to stone and wood.

### Brewing Instructions

1. Soak the gray-green fibers in the clear rain-scented liquid until they contract.
2. Add the silver-backed leaves and crush them against the cauldron wall.
3. Lower a length of dark metallic thread into the mixture and stir until it pulls taut.
4. Dust in the white clarifying crystals.
5. Add the amber syrup in three drops while the thread remains taut.
6. Remove the thread before bottling. The moss must contract before the metal-fiber strand is added or the grip turns brittle.

### Successful Reaction

> The potion crawls up the bottle instead of settling. When the stopper is lifted, the surface clings in a thick green ribbon.

### Wrong-Order Clue

> The liquid sinks like mud and the dark thread snaps into loose fibers. It leaves no tack on glass or stone.

# Potion of Invisibility

**Outcome**: When you drink this potion, you become invisible for 1 hour. The effect ends early if you attack, deal damage, or cast a spell.

### Ingredients

- 2 pinches Powdered Pearl
- 1 measure Moonwater
- 1 pinch Nightshade Berries
- 1 tuft Grave Moss
- 1 pinch Blessed Hart Antler Powder
- 1 pinch Bluecap Spores

**Rationale**: Silver distortion, light-shy moss, and a vanishing spore ring erase the drinker's visible outline.

### Brewing Instructions

1. Expose the gray-green fibers to bright light until they contract.
2. Pour the clear rain-scented liquid over them and keep the cauldron covered.
3. Crush the violet-black fruit beneath the cover.
4. Add the blue powder and breathe across the surface until a blue ring forms.
5. Stir in the smooth ivory powder without lifting the cover.
6. Add the clarifying white crystals last. The spore ring must form before the silver swirls or the outline remains visible.

### Successful Reaction

> The cauldron's rim disappears first, then the potion becomes a hole in the light. Sound continues, but the liquid cannot be seen moving.

### Wrong-Order Clue

> A blue ring hangs in the air and pops like a bubble. The potion settles into a dull gray film that reflects every face around it.

# Potion of Invulnerability

**Outcome**: For 1 minute after you drink this potion, you have resistance to all damage.

### Ingredients

- 2 spoonfuls Troll Blood
- 1 spoonful Black Dragon Scale Powder
- 1 dram Fire Beetle Gland
- 2 pinches Powdered Pearl
- 1 measure Moonwater
- 1 pinch Blessed Hart Antler Powder
- 3 drops Honey of the Sun Bee
- 1 pinch Ironvine Thread

**Rationale**: A self-sealing shell is reinforced by corrosive scale, living heat, silver suspension, and a conductive binding thread.

### Brewing Instructions

1. Pour the clear rain-scented liquid into the cauldron and shake until the sparks form a ring.
2. Stir in the smooth ivory powder until silver ribbons make a continuous shell.
3. Add the vinegar-biting black-green flakes with the iron rod, then wait until the ring stops stinging the metal.
4. Lower in the dark metallic thread and stir until it pulls taut.
5. Add the warm orange-red crystals.
6. Fold in the self-closing copper-smelling paste in two spoonfuls.
7. Drip in the amber syrup.
8. Dust in the white clarifying crystals. The thread must be taut before the paste is added, or the protective shell collapses.

### Successful Reaction

> The brew rises into a dark sphere ringed with silver light. It hardens for one breath, then melts into a perfectly clear potion that makes the bottle feel weightless.

### Wrong-Order Clue

> The cauldron rings like struck iron and throws up a skin of black flakes. The mixture becomes a brittle opaque lump, offering no protection.

# Potion of Battle Prowess

**Outcome**: For 1 minute, you gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls, and your weapon strikes land with brutal certainty.

### Ingredients

- 1 dram Fire Beetle Gland
- 1 pinch Charcoal of Salamander Bone
- 2 drops Wyvern Venom
- 1 spoonful Troll Blood
- 1 measure Moonwater
- 3 drops Honey of the Sun Bee
- 1 sprig Kingsfoil
- 1 pinch Thunderhead Lichen

**Rationale**: Ember, venom, storm spark, and a self-sealing binder sharpen the drinker's force without letting the heat run wild.

### Brewing Instructions

1. Shake the clear rain-scented liquid until its sparks move in a fast ring.
2. Tap the yellow lichen to release one snap of static into that ring.
3. Add the orange-red crystals and the black powder whose red flecks answer flame.
4. Stir in the copper-smelling paste until it closes over the rod.
5. Add the bitter yellow oil in two drops.
6. Drip in the amber syrup.
7. Crush the silver-backed leaves over the cauldron and stir once. The static must be captured before the oil, or the brew loses its edge.

### Successful Reaction

> A sharp crack runs through the bottle. The liquid flashes bronze, and every nearby metal tool points toward it for a moment.

### Wrong-Order Clue

> The brew foams gray and smells of scorched meat. The sparks ground out against the cauldron, leaving a weak, oily potion.
