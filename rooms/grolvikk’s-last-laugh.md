# Grovlikk's Last Laugh

**Room Type:** Comedy Trap / Prize Room  
**Reward:** Green Crystal  
**Primary Threat:** Enhanced Stinking Cloud  
**Solution:** The players must finish the joke by laughing, applauding, heckling, or otherwise playing along with Grovlikk.

## Room Description

> This chamber looks like an old performance room or mock throne hall. The walls are carved with faded scenes of prisoners, guards, nobles, and monsters all laughing at some forgotten joke. Most of the faces have been scratched away.
> In the center of the room stands a life-sized stone statue of a human jester. He wears motley, curled shoes, and a floppy fool’s cap with stone bells hanging from the tips. His face is frozen in a wide, open-mouthed grin.
> One arm is stretched out toward the room, finger extended as if he is singling out someone in the audience. His whole body leans forward slightly, inviting someone to take part in the joke.

This is **Grovlikk**.

Grovlikk was once the personal jester of **Xhal’theris, the Velvet Maw**. He entertained prisoners before they were sent deeper into the dungeon and survived by making even doomed people laugh. His jokes were crude, fearless, and usually aimed at whoever thought themselves too important to be mocked. Then one day, Grovlikk told a joke that did not land. Xhal’theris turned him to stone and left him here as part of the game.

Around the base of the statue is carved:

> A king demands a bow.  
> A priest demands a prayer.  
> A wizard demands an answer.  
> Grovlikk only asks that someone, anyone,  
> help him finish the oldest joke in the world.  

## Trigger

The trap triggers when a creature pulls, twists, breaks, or otherwise strongly manipulates the statue’s extended finger.

When triggered:

- There is a sharp click.
- All doors into the room slam shut and lock.
- For one quiet second, nothing happens.
- Then the statue’s stone eyes begin to water.
- Its grin seems to widen.
- A deep wet gurgle builds inside the pedestal.
- Yellow-green vapor vents from the statue’s backside.

The room begins filling with **Grovlikk’s Offensive Cloud**.

Roll initiative immediately. The trap acts on initiative count 20 each round.

## Grovlikk’s Offensive Cloud

This cloud is a stronger, nastier version of *Stinking Cloud*. It burns the throat, eyes, and lungs while forcing victims to gag and retch.

**Save DC:** 13 Constitution

Any creature that starts its turn inside the cloud must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw.

### On a Failure

- The creature spends its action retching and reeling.
- The creature takes `1d4` poison damage.
- The creature’s movement is halved until the end of its turn.

### On a Success

- The creature still retches, coughs, and gags, but does not lose its action.
- The creature takes half of `1d4` poison damage, minimum 1 poison damage.
- The creature may move and act normally.

Creatures that do not need to breathe have advantage on the saving throw.

Creatures immune to poison automatically succeed against the retching effect but still take 1 poison damage if they remain in the cloud.

## Cloud Expansion

### Initiative Count 20, Round 1

The cloud fills a 10-foot radius around the statue. Only creatures in that area are affected.

### Initiative Count 20, Round 2

The cloud expands to fill half the room.

### Initiative Count 20, Round 3 and Later

The cloud fills the entire room. Every creature in the room is affected at the start of its turn.

The cloud remains until the players solve the room.

## Visibility

Once the cloud fills at least half the room:

- The room is heavily obscured.
- Perception checks relying on sight or smell are made at disadvantage.
- Ranged attacks beyond 5 feet are made at disadvantage.
- Open flames sputter and shed only dim light.

## Important Trap Rule

The trap cannot be stopped by mechanical means.

The players may try to break the statue, jam the doors, block the vents, pick the locks, force the doors, or dispel the gas. These attempts may provide clues or minor temporary benefits, but they do not end the trap and do not open the doors.

Possible adjudication:

- Blocking vents gives advantage on the next Constitution save for creatures near that blocked vent.
- Forcing a door creates a small air gap but does not open the door.
- Breaking part of the statue causes another foul burst; creatures within 10 feet immediately make the cloud saving throw.
- A successful Arcana, Investigation, or Insight check reveals that the magic is tied to the joke, not the gas or doors.

Suggested checks:

- **DC 13 Insight:** The statue seems to be waiting for approval or reaction.
- **DC 13 Investigation:** The new inscription is the only thing that changes after the trap triggers.
- **DC 14 Arcana:** The magic is not centered on the vents or the doors. It is centered on the statue’s performance.
- **DC 15 Performance:** The character realizes Grovlikk wants the bit completed.

## Changed Inscription

After the trap triggers, condensation runs across the base of the statue and reveals new words beneath the original inscription:

> A joke dies when no one laughs.

## Solution: Finish the Joke

The only way to stop the trap is for someone to finish the joke.

The players must laugh at the joke, applaud Grovlikk, heckle him, praise him, or otherwise play along with the performance.

Do not require the exact phrase “pull my finger.” The statue already gave the setup. Grovlikk wants a reaction.

Accepted solutions include:

- A character laughs loudly.
- A character applauds the statue.
- A character says, “Good one, Grovlikk.”
- A character bows and calls him a master fool.
- A character heckles him in a way that still acknowledges the joke.
- A character makes a worse joke in return.
- A bard uses Bardic Performance.
- A character performs an exaggerated fake laugh well enough to sell it.

If the player genuinely commits to the bit, no roll is needed.

If the response is weak, reluctant, or uncertain, use one of these checks:

- **DC 12 Charisma (Performance)** to sell the laugh.
- **DC 14 Charisma (Deception)** to fake appreciation.
- **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion)** to flatter Grovlikk.
- **Bardic Performance** succeeds automatically if the player describes it clearly.

### On a Success

- The statue gives one final wheezing chuckle.
- The cloud stops.
- The doors unlock.
- The hidden compartment opens.

### On a Failure

- The statue’s grin tightens.
- The cloud continues.
- That character has disadvantage on their next Constitution save against the cloud.

## Reward

Once the joke is finished, the gas begins to thin.

> A narrow seam opens across the base of the pedestal. Stone grinds against stone as a small hidden door slides inward, revealing a recessed compartment. Inside rests a **green crystal shard**. Grovlikk’s grin remains fixed above it, as if this was the part of the joke he actually liked.

When viewed through the physical prop **The Scrying Stone**, fine etching appears in the shard:

> Seek the white glow where prayer meets stone.
