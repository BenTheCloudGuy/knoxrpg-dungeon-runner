# Color Coded Trap (Shades of Grey)

**Room Type:** Light Suppression Puzzle / Combat Trap
**Reward:** Supply stash (potions, scrolls, gear, treasure tokens)
**Primary Threat:** Two Sentry Automatons triggered by wrong buttons
**Solution:** Identify the red button without using sight alone, then press it

## Room Description

A small stone chamber, roughly 20 ft × 20 ft. Three round buttons are set evenly across the top of a stone pedestal on the far wall. Two iron-grated alcoves flank the side walls, dark and currently empty. A heavy iron door behind the pedestal — closed.

The room is enchanted to snuff out fire. Torches, lanterns, candles, and other flame-based light sources extinguish the instant they cross the threshold and cannot be relit while inside. Magical light spells (*Light, Dancing Lights, Daylight, Faerie Fire, Continual Flame*) also fail — they fizzle as soon as they are cast or carried inside.

**Darkvision functions normally.** Anyone without darkvision is effectively blind in this room.

## Read Aloud (Entry)

> Your torch dies between one breath and the next. Around you, the rest of the party's lights wink out too — lanterns, candles, anything that burns. The air inside the room is cold and still. Whoever has darkvision can make out the shape of a stone pedestal with three round buttons set into its top. Everyone else is in the dark.

## The Darkvision Trick

This dungeon uses the rule that **darkvision is monochrome** — it sees in shades of grey, not in color. (Note this to the table before the dungeon starts, so it isn't a surprise here.)

This means the three buttons — colored red, green, and yellow in the original construction — all look like **similar shades of grey** to anyone using darkvision. Sight alone is not enough to tell them apart.

## The Engraved Riddle

The front face of the pedestal is carved with a short verse in raised stone letters. A character who runs their hand across the pedestal surface finds the engraving (no check required — it is obvious to the touch). Reading it tactilely takes about 30 seconds of careful tracing. Anyone literate in Common can read it. Other languages require a Comprehend Languages or similar effect.

> Three buttons wait — Green, Yellow, and Red.
> Envy rots slow.
> Fear shakes the bone.
> One alone burns with wrath.
>
> Press what rages. Or else you're dead.

**Solving the riddle:**

- *Envy* is traditionally green ("green with envy").
- *Fear* is traditionally yellow ("yellow-bellied," "yellow streak").
- *Wrath, fury, "seeing red,"* *the burning one* — red.
The red button is the answer.

**Why the riddle exists:** It is the *which color do I press?* solution that works independently of darkness. Even a party that defeats or bypasses the light suppression (clever workaround, a light source the GM didn't anticipate, the Scrying Stone, etc.) still sees three colored buttons with no reason to pick one over the others. The riddle is the in-room clue that says: *press red.* Touch, sound, and magical detection are bonus paths; the riddle is the one that's always available to anyone who thinks to feel the pedestal.

## How To Identify the Red Button

Multiple paths. Let the players figure out their own approach. Any of these works:

### 1. The Scrying Stone (the designed solution)

Looking at the buttons through the Scrying Stone reveals their true colors. The red one glows clearly.

### 2. Touch (Investigation DC 12)

The red button is faintly warm. The green and yellow are stone-cold. Anyone who presses a hand to all three notices the difference.

### 3. Sound (Perception DC 14)

Tapping each button produces a soft hum. The red button hums at a higher, brighter pitch. The other two are flat and dull.

### 4. Magical Detection

*Detect Magic* reveals all three buttons radiate magic. The red button radiates **transmutation** (the door mechanism). The other two radiate **conjuration** (the sentries waiting in the alcoves).

### 5. Preview the Room

A player can light a torch just outside the threshold and step partially in. The torch dies, but if they hold the moment in their memory, they may have caught a glimpse of color before the light went out. Investigation or Perception DC 13 to recall which button was red.

### 6. A Clue From Elsewhere

Drop a hint somewhere earlier in the dungeon — graffiti in a hallway, a fragment in a previous room, a Xhal'theris taunt: *"Push the warm one."* GM choice.

## Pressing a Button

### Red Button (correct)

The door behind the pedestal slides open with a low grinding sound. The light suppression in the room briefly lifts — torches can be relit and held inside while the door is open. Beyond the door is the supply stash.

### Wrong Button (green or yellow)

The corresponding alcove grate slides open. A **Sentry Automaton** steps out and attacks immediately. The button itself doesn't reset — it stays pressed and inert. Each wrong button activates its sentry exactly once.

If both wrong buttons are pressed, both sentries are loose simultaneously. The party can still press the red button at any point during the fight to open the door.

## Sentry Automaton

A 5-ft-tall humanoid construct of brass and dark iron with two glowing crystal eyes. Silent. Single-minded.

- **AC** 15 (natural armor)
- **HP** 35
- **Speed** 30 ft
- **Saves** Str +3, Dex +2
- **Skills** Perception +4
- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft (true color), passive Perception 14
- **Damage Immunities** Poison, psychic
- **Condition Immunities** Charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned
- **Languages** Understands Common, cannot speak
- **CR** 2

**Antimagic Susceptibility.** The sentry is incapacitated while in the area of an antimagic field. If targeted by *Dispel Magic*, the sentry makes a Con save against the caster's spell save DC; on a failure it falls unconscious for 1 minute.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack.** The sentry makes two slam attacks.
- **Slam.** Melee weapon attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft, one target. Hit: 1d8+2 bludgeoning damage.

**Tactics.** Sentries target whoever pressed their button first, then the nearest enemy. They will pursue into adjacent rooms if their target flees. They are not subtle — they walk in straight lines and hit hard.

## The Supply Stash

Behind the open door, a small alcove containing:

- 3× minor healing potions
- 1× scroll of *Daylight*
- 1× scroll of *Faerie Fire*
- 1× hooded lantern + 4 flasks of oil
- 50 ft hempen rope
- A small pouch with 2 treasure tokens
- A folded note with a Scrying Stone clue pointing to another crystal (GM picks)

## GM Notes

- **Make the light snuffing immediate and visible.** Players will instinctively try to light a torch — describe it dying *the moment* it crosses the threshold. This sells the puzzle.
- **Don't punish creative thinking.** If players come up with a fourth or fifth way to identify the red button (sniffing for heat, dropping water on each one to see which steams, having a Tiefling with *Hellish Rebuke* warm one up, etc.) — let it work.
- **Brute force is valid.** A party of 8-12 can absorb both sentries if they just want to press all three buttons. That's a fine choice — they're trading resources for time. The fight is meant to be punishing, not impossible.
- **The door stays open after solving.** Once the red button is pressed, the door is open for the rest of the dungeon. Other players who arrive later can walk straight through.
- **The light suppression turns off when the door opens.** Players can finally light a torch inside — make a moment of it. They see the colored buttons properly for the first time. Optional: the wrong buttons are colored *exactly* the same shade of grey if examined now, as a final taunt.
- **The Scrying Stone is the elegant solution.** If the party never uses it here, drop a heavier hint in the next room: *"Have you been looking through the stone? It sees more than you do."*

## Xhal'theris Flavor

On entry, after lights go out:

> *"Oh, dear. So little light. I do hope you packed your wits and not just your candles."*

On a wrong button press:

> *"Wrong. The dolls would so love to play."*

When the second sentry is also released:

> *"Two dolls! And one of you to share between them. How festive."*

When the red button is finally pressed:

> *"There. Was that so hard? Take your prize. Try not to die before the next door."*
