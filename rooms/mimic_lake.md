# Mimic Lake

**Room Type:** Exploration / Stealth / Combat Encounter
**Reward:** Blue Crystal, healing potion, scroll, spell components, treasure tokens
**Primary Threat:** Freezing water, an awakening lake predator, and one very patient mimic
**Solution:** Cross the lake quietly, deal with the island chests carefully, claim the Blue Crystal, and leave before the lake wakes up

![Mimic Lake](../images/rooms/mimic_lake.png)

## Room Description

The lake terrain is rendered on the **42" monitor spanning both tables.** Custom foam forms the rough shores. Small printable stone islands serve as momentary safe spots in the water. The raft is a physical token the GM moves across the monitor.

**Read Aloud (on entry):**

> The tunnel opens onto a vast underground lake. The ceiling vaults high overhead, lost in shadow, and the black water stretches further than your torchlight can find. The surface is mirror-still. The air bites cold enough that every breath fogs in front of you.
> A crude raft is tied to the stone shore at your feet, water lapping silently against its sides. Across the lake, maybe thirty yards out, a small island rises from the surface — a low stone shelf, and on it a cluster of chests and crates, half-hidden in the dark.
> From somewhere far to the south you hear water. A muted, constant roar — the sound of a waterfall, distant but huge, echoing off the cavern walls until you can't tell where the room ends.
> The lake is silent in every other way. *Too silent.*

## The Cold

The lake is glacial meltwater that has seeped down through cracks in the stone over centuries. It is *cold enough to kill.*

- **A creature fully or partially submerged** in the lake makes a **DC 12 Constitution save at the start of each of its turns.**
- **Failure:** Takes **1d4 cold damage** and gains the **Stiffened** condition (cumulative):
  - **1 stack:** Speed (including swim speed) reduced by 10 ft. DC 14
  - **2 stacks:** Speed reduced by 20 ft total. Disadvantage on Dex saves. DC 16
  - **3 stacks:** Speed 0. The creature can no longer swim. It sinks DC 18. Begins suffocating per standard drowning rules.
- **Success:** No damage, no new stack.
- **Recovery:** Stiffened stacks do NOT clear automatically when the creature leaves the water. Removing stacks requires either:
  - Must get out of Cold/Wet Clothes
  - CON saves on subsequent rounds even after getting out the water or take 2HP Cold Damage until dried off and warming started. 
  - 15 minutes near a fire or heat source (clears 1 stack)
  - A *healing* spell of 1st level or higher (clears 1 stack)
  - A healing potion (clears 1 stack, in addition to its HP)
  - A character-driven solution the GM judges effective (vigorous rubbing, body heat, magic, etc.)

Stiffened is a punishing condition by design — anyone who falls in the lake and doesn't get out fast is a real liability for the rest of the dungeon.

## The Current

The entire lake flows **south, toward the waterfall.** The current is not violent in the open water, but it is constant and unforgiving. Anything floating freely — a body, a dropped pack, a raft no one is poling, an unconscious PC — drifts steadily south every round, and the closer it gets to the falls, the harder it is to fight back.

- **Ambient drift (anywhere on the lake).** At the end of each round, any uncontrolled object or unconscious / unswimming creature in the water moves **10 ft south.** A conscious swimmer can hold position by spending their movement actively swimming against the current and succeeding on a **DC 10 Strength (Athletics)** check at the start of their turn. On a failure, they still drift 5 ft south.
- **Swimming with the current (southbound):** Effective swim speed +10 ft per round. No check required.
- **Swimming against the current (northbound):** Effective swim speed −10 ft per round, and the swimmer must succeed on a **DC 12 Athletics** check or make no progress that round.
- **Stepping stones break the current locally.** A swimmer who reaches a stepping stone is no longer drifting while in contact with it (the stone interrupts the flow in its lee, about 5 ft of calmer water).
- **Approaching the falls.** The pull intensifies into a true pull within 30 ft of the lip — see *The Waterfall* for the danger-zone rules. Anyone drifting freely south will reach that zone eventually.

This matters most for: (1) a PC who falls off the raft, (2) the raft itself if no one is poling it, and (3) any body or item dropped into the water. Players should feel the lake *pulling* on everything south.

## The Noise (The Alert Track)

Something lives in the deep water. It is hibernating. It hunts by vibration and sound. The party can cross the lake safely if they are quiet, but every loud thing they do moves them closer to a fight they do not want.

Track the lake's alertness on three stages:

| Stage | State | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| **0 — Calm** | Sleeping | Black mirror surface. No sign of life. Starting state. |
| **1 — Stirring** | Roused | A long, slow ripple crosses the lake from somewhere deep. The water grows a degree colder. Bubbles rise here and there. *Last warning.* |
| **2 — Hunting** | Awake | **The Drowner surfaces. Combat begins.** Roll initiative. |

**Noise events that advance the track by 1 stage:**

- A character falls into the lake (any reason)
- A weapon strike, thrown stone, or projectile lands in the lake water
- Combat noise from the island (the Mimic fight automatically advances the track each round of combat)
- A spell with verbal, somatic-with-flash, or audible components cast on the raft or shore
- Rowing the raft faster than careful pace (Dash action)
- Shouting, panicked screaming, or loud arguments above a whisper

**Noise events that do NOT advance the track:**

- Calm conversation in whispers
- Careful rowing at half pace
- Silent spellcasting (Subtle Spell metamagic, somatic-only with no flash, etc.)
- Stealth-checked movement that succeeds DC 12

**GM advice:** Don't be stingy. The lake should advance to Stirring within the first crossing if the party is doing anything more than tiptoeing. Stirring is a *warning,* not a punishment — give the party one round to react, change behavior, or hurry. If they make any noise-triggering action while at Stirring, go straight to Hunting. The Drowner appears at the nearest deep-water location to the noisemaker.

## The Raft

A crude wooden platform, 10 ft × 10 ft, with rope handles and a pole/paddle for movement. Requires a designated **pole-handler** at all times — if no one is poling, the raft drifts south on the current (see *The Current*).

- **Safe capacity:** 4–5 PCs (Medium creatures). Over 5 is overloaded.
- **Speed:** 15 ft per round at careful pace (no noise gain). 30 ft per round at Dash pace (advances Alert Track).
- **Direction vs. current:**
  - **Southbound (with the current):** +5 ft per round to effective speed. No Athletics check required from the poler.
  - **East–West crossing** (the typical route to the island): no speed change, but the poler must succeed on a **DC 8 Athletics** check at the start of each round or the raft drifts 5 ft south instead of holding its line.
  - **Northbound (against the current):** −5 ft per round to effective speed, and the poler must succeed on a **DC 12 Athletics** check or the raft makes no progress that round.
  - **Unmanned raft:** Drifts 10 ft south at the end of each round on the ambient current. If the poler is knocked overboard, killed, or pulled under, this happens *immediately the next round.*
- **Overloading:** 6+ Medium creatures on the raft requires a **DC 13 Dexterity (acrobatics) check from the pole-handler at the start of each round.** Failure: the raft tips. Everyone on it makes a **DC 14 Dex save** or falls into the water (auto-Stage advance + start taking Cold damage next turn).
- **Targeting the raft:** The Drowner can attack the raft itself (AC 12, HP 30). At 0 HP the raft breaks apart and everyone goes in.
- **Disembarking:** Stepping from raft to island or shore takes a normal move. Stepping wrong (e.g., raft drifts) requires a DC 10 Dex save to avoid falling in.

## The Drowner

The lake's apex predator. A pale, eel-like creature roughly 25 ft long, milky-white, blind, with a single faintly glowing bioluminescent lure dangling from its forehead. It hunts by tremorsense in the water and is utterly silent until it strikes.

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| **AC** | 14 (natural armor) |
| **HP** | 95 (10d12+30) |
| **Speed** | 5 ft / **Swim 60 ft** |
| **STR** | 18 (+4) |
| **DEX** | 14 (+2) |
| **CON** | 16 (+3) |
| **INT** | 3 (-4) |
| **WIS** | 12 (+1) |
| **CHA** | 6 (-2) |
| **Saves** | Str +6, Con +5 |
| **Skills** | Perception +3, Stealth +4 (in water) |
| **Damage Resistances** | Cold; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks while in deep water |
| **Condition Immunities** | Blinded, prone |
| **Senses** | Blindsight 30 ft, **Tremorsense 120 ft (in water only),** blind beyond |
| **Languages** | — |
| **Challenge** | 5 (1,800 XP) |

**Cold Aura.** Any creature that starts its turn within 10 ft of the Drowner (in the water) takes **1d6 cold damage** and has disadvantage on Constitution saves against the lake's Cold effect that round.

**Amphibious.** The Drowner can hold its breath indefinitely and breathes both water and air, but its swim speed in air drops to 5 ft (it must drag itself along the surface).

**Surprise Lunge.** On the first round of combat, the Drowner has advantage on its attack against any creature that did not yet know it was there.

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** The Drowner makes one Bite attack and one Constrict attack.
- **Bite.** Melee weapon attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft, one target. Hit: **2d8+4 piercing damage.**
- **Constrict.** Melee weapon attack: +6 to hit, reach 10 ft, one target not already constricted. Hit: **1d10+4 bludgeoning damage** and the target is **grappled (escape DC 14).** At the end of the Drowner's next turn, the grappled target is dragged 15 ft toward the deep water and pulled below the surface (start drowning unless they can hold their breath).
- **Drown (action while grappling).** The Drowner pulls a grappled creature fully underwater. The creature starts suffocating per the standard suffocation rules and remains grappled and underwater until the grapple ends.

### Tactics

- Targets the noisiest creature first (the one who triggered the alert track to Hunting).
- Bite, constrict, drag, drown — in that order.
- If reduced to 1/4 HP, dives deep and retreats. Returns in 10 rounds unless the party has left the lake.
- Stepping stones and the island are safe — the Drowner cannot leave the water effectively. A PC who reaches a stone (any of the small printable outcrops) is out of reach until they re-enter the water.

## The Island

A low stone shelf rising about 3 ft out of the water. Roughly 15 ft × 20 ft of usable surface. Cluttered with old chests and crates left here by previous victims.

**Read Aloud (on disembarking):**

> The island is bone-dry, which is strange. The lake doesn't lap up onto it. Four chests sit in a rough half-circle: two iron-banded, one plain wood, one carved with old runes you cannot read. Behind them, a low stone pedestal — and on the pedestal, a fist-sized **blue crystal** that catches your torchlight and throws it back, sharp and cold.
> One of the chests is breathing. *Slowly.*

**On the island:**

- **4 chests in total.** Three are real; one is the **Mimic.**
  - **Real Chest 1 (iron-banded):** Contains a **healing potion** and a **scroll of [GM's choice — suggest *Cure Wounds* or *Healing Word*].**
  - **Real Chest 2 (iron-banded):** Contains **spell components** worth 50 gp total — pinch of phosphorus, vial of mercury, three small pearls, etc. Anything the party's casters would actually want.
  - **Real Chest 3 (rune-carved):** Contains **2 treasure tokens** and **20 gp in mixed coin.**
  - **The Mimic (plain wood):** Looks the most innocent. See *The Mimic* below.
- **The Blue Crystal pedestal.** The crystal sits on a low stone column behind the chests. Lifting it is a free action — but the party has to *get past the chests* to reach it.

**Detection options for spotting the Mimic without touching:**

- **Detect Magic:** All four chests radiate faint conjuration (illusion masking the Mimic). The Mimic radiates noticeably stronger.
- **Investigation DC 15:** The plain wooden chest is the only one without dust on its lid. The others have decades of grit; this one was used recently — by something.
- **Perception DC 16:** A character listening carefully hears slow, wet, organic breathing from the wooden chest. (Auto-pass for any character within 5 ft of it.)
- **The Scrying Stone:** Looking at the chests through the stone reveals the Mimic clearly — its true form pulsing under the wooden shell.

## The Mimic

Standard Monster Manual mimic, no modifications.

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| **AC** | 12 (natural armor) |
| **HP** | 58 (9d8+18) |
| **Speed** | 15 ft |
| **STR** | 17 (+3) |
| **DEX** | 12 (+1) |
| **CON** | 15 (+2) |
| **Skills** | Stealth +5 |
| **Damage Immunities** | Acid |
| **Condition Immunities** | Prone |
| **Senses** | Darkvision 60 ft |
| **Challenge** | 2 (450 XP) |

**Shapechanger.** The mimic can use its action to polymorph into an object or back into its true amorphous form. Its statistics are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying is not transformed. It reverts to its true form if it dies.

**Adhesive (Object Form Only).** The mimic adheres to anything that touches it. A Huge or smaller creature stuck to the mimic is also grappled by it (escape DC 13). Ability checks made to escape this grapple have disadvantage.

**False Appearance (Object Form Only).** While the mimic remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary object.

**Grappler.** The mimic has advantage on attack rolls against any creature grappled by it.

### Actions

- **Pseudopod.** Melee weapon attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft, one target. Hit: **1d8+3 bludgeoning damage.** If the mimic is in object form, the target is subjected to its Adhesive trait.
- **Bite.** Melee weapon attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft, one target. Hit: **1d8+3 piercing damage plus 1d8 acid damage.**

### Tactics on the Island

- Holds object form until a PC touches it OR opens it. First touch = adhesive = grappled.
- Fights to kill the stuck character first, then pivots to the next nearest target.
- The Mimic is **CR 2 against a level 3 party of 8–12.** It will fold in 2–3 rounds of focused fire. The danger is **noise.** Every round of mimic combat advances the Alert Track. A 3-round mimic fight = Drowner shows up just as the Mimic dies.

## The Waterfall

A wide curtain of falling water at the south end of the cavern, pouring over a stone lip into the unknown. The lake's ambient southward current (see *The Current*) intensifies into a true pull as the water approaches the lip.

- **Visual telegraph:** From the raft, a player can see standing waves and white water 30–40 ft from the falls. PCs who row into that zone are in trouble.
- **Intensified pull (within 30 ft of the lip):** This zone overrides the ambient current rules. Any creature or raft within 30 ft of the lip is pulled **20 ft toward the falls at the end of each round** (instead of the normal 10 ft drift). Resisting requires a **DC 14 Athletics** check (swimming) or a **DC 13 Strength** check (poling the raft). Each success reduces that round's pull by 10 ft; two helpers poling together can cancel it entirely.
- **Going over:**
  - **Voluntary controlled descent:** A PC who deliberately surfs the falls makes a **DC 15 Athletics or Acrobatics** check. **Success:** They drop into the lower cave room's pool with **1d6 bludgeoning** damage (they stuck the landing). **Failure:** They take **4d6 bludgeoning** damage and arrive prone and exhausted.
  - **Involuntary plunge:** A creature swept over without preparation takes **4d6 bludgeoning** damage on the rocks below and arrives in the pool of whatever room the waterfall feeds into. **Note: the destination room must be designed before this becomes a live escape route.** Until then, treat the waterfall as a one-way trip the party can choose but the GM rules irrevocable.

## GM Notes

- **Sell the silence.** Before the first crossing, describe the lake's stillness over and over. The party needs to feel that noise is dangerous before they're told it is.
- **Reward whispering.** If players RP whispering at the table, give them in-fiction advantage — auto-success on quiet rowing checks, no Alert Track gain for that round, etc. Make the table go quiet by mirroring the lake.
- **The Mimic is a noise trap.** It exists to make the party fight on the island. The fight noise is what wakes the Drowner. Players who realize this can try to *avoid* the fight — sneak past the chests, lift the crystal, and bolt. Reward that thinking.
- **The Drowner is supposed to feel inevitable.** It probably wakes up. That's fine. The lesson is *how much trouble are you in by the time it does?* A party that's already on the island with the crystal in hand is in good shape. A party still on the raft with two PCs in the water is in real trouble.
- **Use the stepping stones.** When the Drowner attacks, drop a printable stone island into the water near a struggling PC. Make it a Dex save (DC 12) to scramble onto it. This rewards quick thinking and turns the room from "you're all dead" to "make a stand."
- **The waterfall is bait.** A panicked party will look at it as an escape route. Let them try — but only if the destination room exists yet. Otherwise, describe the falls as obviously suicidal and treat it as scenery.
- **The 42" monitor is the star.** Light the room with the monitor's glow and dim the rest of the table. Cue ambient sound: a slow water track for Calm, deeper bubbling water for Stirring, a low subsonic rumble for Hunting. The cold-cavern atmosphere does half the work.

## Xhal'theris Flavor

On entry, as the party first sees the lake:

> *"Oh, you'll like this one. So quiet. So restful. Just don't make any sudden movements. Or sounds. Or interesting thoughts."*

When the first PC touches the water:

> *"Mm. That's cold, isn't it. The kind of cold that remembers being a glacier."*

When the Alert Track reaches Stirring:

> *"Did you hear that? No? Try again. Listen with the part of you that knows it's being watched."*

When the Drowner surfaces:

> *"There she is. Lovely, isn't she. She's been so lonely."*

When the Mimic strikes:

> *"Ohhh. The chest. Yes. Always the chest. You'd think one of you would learn."*

When the Blue Crystal is claimed:

> *"One down. Beautiful. Tuck it somewhere safe. The water has a long memory."*

When a PC dies in the lake:

> *"Don't reach for them. The lake keeps what it takes. That one's mine now."*
