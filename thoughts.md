## Lava Room 

- This is where the **Red Crystal** will be located
- Players will encounter Fire Elementals and Imps. 

## Reading Rainbow Room

- This room will have the floor carved with letters.. The players must step on the first letter of each color of he rainbow in order.. ROYGBIV

That stands for the rainbow colors in order:
- Red
- Orange
- Yellow
- Green
- Blue
- Indigo
- Violet

Safely crossing the room will allow the players to access the Artificers Workshop with supplies and it's Sentry. 

## Artificers Lair

- Guarded by Sentry (Construct)
- Contains the Purple Crystal
- Secret Room. 
- Once Sentry is defeated - can be used as a safe haven for rests. 
- Artificers lair has several rooms with traps, puzzles, and dangers. 

## Mirror Trap

- Players find a mirror in the room with word above it. If they say the word their transported and trapped in the mirror and the creature in the mirror is released. Give the player a card telling them about the creature and let them know how to act.
