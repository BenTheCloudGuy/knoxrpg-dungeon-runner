# Dungeon Areas #

This dungeon is full of rooms, puzzles, locked doors, monsters, treasure, and bosses. It is a mix of Dungeon Crawler Carl, Running Man, and Resident Evil!

## Northern Cells ##

> This is one of two Cells where Runners will start. Two cells of equal size with a key just outside reach, picking up the key off the hook causing the ceiling to start grinding down.. The Lobby has Scrying Stone.

- Cells
  - TRAP: Crushing Cieling
- Last Laugh
  - TRAP: Pull my Finger/Poison Cloud
  - TREASURE: Scroll of Stinking Cloud
- Item Room
  - TREASURE:
    - Starter Equipment and Coins.
- Lobby
  - TRAP: Main Door is trapped -> Spike Trap

## GOBLIN TUNNELS ##

> Goblin Tunnels are small and narrow dirt passages. Hidden through out the tunnels are traps and murder holes where the Goblins will use to escape and hit and run the players.

- Goblin Village
- Goblin Tunnels
- Full of Goblins attacking any that enter.
- TREASURE:
  - GobStoppers
  - Crystal (Green)
  - Magic Items
- Monsters:
  - Goblins have mix of weapons including -> GobStoppers (Goblin Grenades)
  - Hobgoblin - King GobGob drops crystal

## CAVERNS ##

> The Caverns wrap around the Northern Cells and connects the various areas.

- Southern Large Open Cavern
  - Treasure Room (Locked by door)
  - TRAP: Falling Stalagtites (Random)
  - TRAP: Poisoned Well. Hidden treasure in well bucket.
- Long Tunnel
  - TRAP: Arrows Traps - These do little damage and are a red herring to convince players to go into the tunnel where the real trap lives - rolling stone.
  - TRAP: Sticky Floors - difficult terrain (1/2 movement)
  - TRAP: Rolling Stones - Crushing Stone (10d6) and moves 45' per round. Small players can atempt to drop into corner of wall and let stone roll past them (Dex Save). Allow players to be creative in stopping this trap > Such as wall of Force.
- Goblin Entrance Room (3 entrances) - Goblin Door
- Monsters:
  - Slime Monsters
  - Random Golblins

## Abyssal Bridge ##

> Bridge crosses over a bottomless abyss. There is a strong wind blowing up from the endless abyss below..  

- Northern Goblin Entrance and Bridge over abyss
  - Treasure on Cliffs in the hands of of the cage. Crystal (Blue) along with handout explaining the crystals and clue to Floor Puzzle and a magical ring.
  
- TRAP:
  - Bridge has weight limit and will snap if exceeded.
  - Bridge is wood and will burn/catch fire if hit with fire/acid.
  - The bridge will be rolling due to wind requiring Dex saves to cross safely. Rolling a 1 on save throws the player off into the Abyss, other wise a failed roll knocks player off their feet and they can't move any further. On next turn they need to make another save, on a second fail they fall off on roll of 1-5, then 1-10, on a 4th fail they fall.
- MONSTER:
  - Bat Swarm.

## SPIDER KEEP ##

> This area is controlled by the Spider Queen. She is powerful Scorceress/Dryder and fallen priestess of Lloth.

- Treasure Room (locked door)
- Small side area (lower)
- Teleporter Room (Rainbow Road)
- TRAP:
  - Slime Door to Slime Monster. Slime door does Acid damage and will attempt to hold/engulf anyone attempting to pass through. If touched will start to wrap around object burning and disolving it in a few rounds.
- Monsters:
  - Large Spiders
  - Spider Swarms
  - Slime Monster

## SOUTHERN CELLS ##

> This is where the players will start in this area. 3 Cells with buttons on the wall. The players must solve the puzzle to open one of the doors. This will allow those players to open other doors. There is a console in the center of each cell 2' tall. The ceilding are 8' tall (meaning after 6 failures the terminal is not accessible).

- Scrying Stone Here
- TRAP:
  - 3 Prinsoner trap. When the players enter the wrong sequence on puzzle the cieling will lower one step. (Use d6 to track failures per Cell). Each cell will have a clue to another cells puzzle. The players will need to figure out which is which.
