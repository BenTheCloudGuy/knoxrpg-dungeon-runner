# Decision: Read-aloud boxes contain only player-perceivable info

**Author:** Marcille (Narrator & Voice)
**Date:** 2026-10-02
**Requested by:** Ben

## Decision

Read-aloud / boxed text contains ONLY what the player characters can perceive at that moment with their own senses. Read-aloud must NOT contain:

- the outcome of a hidden trap or hazard (a stone tips, a floor is a pit, gas is coming, a beam will fire, a chest is a mimic, a boulder will roll);
- hidden creatures or ambushers the party has not yet seen or heard;
- secret doors, hidden items, or anything gated behind a check the party has not made;
- mechanics, DCs, saves, damage, or GM intent.

Fair perceivable cues stay (for example "the stones ride low", "the floor is oily", "the door is choked with cobwebs", "the wild magic is visibly crackling"). Trigger/reveal read-aloud that fires at the moment something happens (the trap going off after it triggers, a boss revealing herself when she attacks) is correct and stays. The leak to kill is read-aloud that pre-warns players of something still hidden.

When a leaked fact is useful to the GM and not already written elsewhere in the room, move it into a `> [!NOTE] GM NOTE` or the existing trap mechanics. Never lose a mechanic.

## Scope applied

Swept all six room files. Fixed 4 read-aloud leaks (Dungeon-Right R6 Hopscotch entering, Dungeon-Left D9 Serpents Lair entering, crypts entering, GoblinTunnels Area 4 entering). The already-corrected R6 "crossing the stones" box is the reference model and was left as-is. In all four cases the secret already lived in GM prose / TRAP / puzzle / monster blocks, so no mechanic moved or was lost.
