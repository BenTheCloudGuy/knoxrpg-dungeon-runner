# Chilchuck - History

## Core Context

**Project:** knoxrpg-dungeon-runner - "The Vault of the Starving Mind"
**Owner:** Ben Mitchell (BenTheCloudGuy)
**Created:** 2026-05-30
**My role:** Encounter & Stat Block Designer - 5e 2024 stat blocks, trap mechanics, encounter math

## Party assumptions

- 8-12 PCs at level 3, 40 HP cap, no long rests, max 3 short rests at 1 HD each.
- Action economy is heavily skewed toward the players at this scale.
- Lethality is intended.

## Summarized older entries through 2026-09-27

- Keep the survival-loot balance bar: no recurring healing, at-will hard control, flight, teleport, save-or-die effects, or casual anti-Mind-Flayer counters. Safe loot stays finite, consumable, or capped at the +1 band.
- The locked loot model matters for encounter assumptions: characters start with clothing only, scavenge Item Cards, and permanent magic items come through Treasure Goblin vendors rather than room treasure.
- Named spell-scroll card text was audited against 2024 rules. Guidance is Action and Concentration, Cure Wounds and Mass Healing Word use 2024 healing dice and Abjuration, and third-level scrolls use DC 15 or +7.
- Berhan Voss, Hob Gob, Drider Priestess, Statue, Sysuul Spawn, Giant Slime, goblins, zone creatures, lizardfolk, forge creatures, and elemental or spider reskins were recorded as monster work leading to the current official-stat policy.
- The official-stat policy is durable: use official 2024 Monster Manual stats first, use 2014 stats in 2024 format when needed, and calibrate with creature choice and quantity rather than custom stat tuning unless Ben supplies hand-authored numbers.
- Stat-block files no longer include DM Notes. Placement, treasure, tactics, Crystal Shard triggers, and campaign context belong in room files.
- Character sheet rules work established the no-rest doubling rule for printed sheets: double per-rest, per-day, and spell-slot resources, but do not double passive, at-will, per-turn, reaction, HP, attacks, DCs, or bonuses.
