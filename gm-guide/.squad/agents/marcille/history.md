## Learnings

### Read-aloud leak sweep (all six room files)
Ben flagged that some read-aloud boxes pre-warn players of still-hidden traps, ambushers, or secrets. Rule locked: read-aloud contains ONLY what the PCs can perceive at that moment with their own senses. It must not state the outcome of a hidden trap/hazard, name unseen ambushers, reveal secret doors or gated items, or expose mechanics/DCs/GM intent. Keep fair perceivable cues (for example "stones ride low", "oily floor", "cobwebbed door", visible glowing wild magic, audible crackling). Trigger/reveal boxes ("when the trap fires", "when the boss reveals herself") are correct and stay, because they fire at the moment the thing happens.

Model correction (from Ben, R6 Hopscotch crossing box, leave as-is): keep "ride a little low in the glow", drop "land on the wrong one and it tips out from under you".

Fixes made this pass (4 leaks, read-aloud wording only; every secret already lived in GM prose/TRAP/puzzle/monster blocks so nothing was moved or lost):
- `rooms/Dungeon-Right.md` R6 Hopscotch "entering": removed "they look like they would tip the instant you trusted your weight to them" (leaked the tip-and-dump trap outcome). Left the already-corrected "crossing the stones" box untouched per Ben.
- `rooms/Dungeon-Left.md` D9 Serpents Lair "entering": removed "Something long and scaled shifts in the shadows, and you get the clear sense you are already being watched" (pre-revealed the hidden ambusher Vos'sykriss before his reveal box).
- `rooms/crypts.md` "entering": removed "and nothing in here should be moving. Some of it is moving anyway" (pre-warned that the dead are animate before the skeleton/wraith reveal boxes).
- `rooms/GoblinTunnels.md` Area 4 "entering": replaced "This is where the goblins have been rolling their boulders down from" with a perceivable cue ("the dirt of the incline is scored and gouged"); kept the visible murder hole. Leaked "a boulder will roll."

Verified clean (no change needed): TheCaverns.md and GoblinCamp.md read-alouds all describe visible scenes; hidden slime/Drider/kamikaze flyers/plants are only shown in trigger boxes. Borderline cues judged FAIR and kept: C2 "the bridge shifts the moment you put weight on it" (felt, not hidden), C3 "you catch movement behind it" (a perceived glimpse, not an unseen ambush), R7 "something made of fire and running rock is moving" down a passage (glowing elemental visible at distance), GoblinCamp A3 open fleshy plant ringed with bones (plant is in plain sight, consequence not stated).

Design principle for future rooms: write the "entering" box as the visible establishing shot only. Put the ambush/trap payload in a separate "Read Aloud (when X)" trigger box or a GM NOTE. Do not let the establishing shot spend the surprise.
