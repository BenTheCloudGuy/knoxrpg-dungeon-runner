# Grovlikk's Last Laugh

Grovlikk was one my most prized possessions. He was my jester, my one reliable instrument for cutting panic out of a room. He could humiliate a duke, soften a mob, and make shackled prisoners forget they were doomed for the length of a breath. Grovlikk loved nothing more than to bring laughter to his audience. 

Once he mocked me to my face, and the whole chamber looked at him, stunned. He flashed that crooked grin of his and he held the room in his hand and waited for the expected laughter and applause. 

No one dared too laugh... 

As payment for a joke in such poor taste, I turned him to stone and set him in my little game. So that he may continue to entertain guests forever. Never let it be said that I do not have a sense of humor.

So as you stand before him, give him what he wants.
