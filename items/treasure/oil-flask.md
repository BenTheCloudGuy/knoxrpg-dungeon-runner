# Oil (flask)

- **Description:** A flask of lamp oil.
- **Use:** As an action, throw at a target within 20 feet as a ranged attack to douse it. If the target takes Fire damage before the oil dries after 1 minute, it takes an extra 5 Fire damage. You can instead pour it to cover a 5-foot square.
- **Cost:** 1 gp
- **Weight:** 1 lb.
- **Source:** phb, br
