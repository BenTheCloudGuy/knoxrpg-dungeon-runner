# Torch

- **Description:** A wooden rod topped with pitch-soaked cloth.
- **Use:** Burns for 1 hour, casting Bright Light in a 20-foot radius and Dim Light for another 20 feet. As an improvised melee weapon it deals 1 Fire damage.
- **Cost:** 1 gp
- **Weight:** 1 lb.
- **Source:** phb-2024, free-rules, phb, br
