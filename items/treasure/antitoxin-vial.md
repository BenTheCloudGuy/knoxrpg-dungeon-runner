# Antitoxin (vial)

- **Description:** A vial of medicinal fluid that fights poison.
- **Use:** As a Bonus Action, drink it to gain Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Poisoned condition for 1 hour.
- **Cost:** 50 gp
- **Weight:** -
