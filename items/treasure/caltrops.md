# Caltrops

- **Description:** A bag of spiked metal jacks.
- **Use:** As an action, spread these across a 5-foot square. A creature that enters makes a DC 15 Dexterity save or takes 1 Piercing damage and its Speed drops to 0 until the start of its next turn.
- **Cost:** 1 GP
- **Weight:** 2 lb.
- **Source:** phb-2024, free-rules
