# Hunting Trap

- **Description:** A spring-loaded jaw of steel teeth.
- **Use:** As an action, set the trap. A creature that steps on it makes a DC 13 Dexterity save or takes 1d4 Piercing damage and stops moving. A DC 13 Strength check frees it.
- **Cost:** 5 GP
- **Weight:** 25 lb.
- **Source:** phb-2024, free-rules, phb, br
