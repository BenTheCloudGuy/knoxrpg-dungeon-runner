# Alchemist’s fire (flask)

- **Description:** A sticky, flammable liquid in a breakable flask.
- **Use:** As an action, throw at a target within 20 feet as a ranged attack. On a hit, the target takes 1d4 Fire damage and starts burning, taking 1d4 Fire at the start of each of its turns. A creature can end the burning by taking an action to make a DC 10 Dexterity check.
- **Cost:** 50 gp
- **Weight:** 1 lb.
- **Source:** phb
