# Artificer's Toolkit

- **Description:** A leather toolroll with fine gears, wrenches, files, calipers, and small tinkering tools.
- **Use:** You have Advantage on ability checks made to craft, repair, tinker with, or disable mechanisms and items.
- **Uses:** The toolkit contains 10 uses. Each use is one crafting, repair, tinker, or disable attempt. Mark off a use each time. When all 10 are spent the toolkit is used up.
- **Cost:** 25 GP
- **Source:** Custom - The Vault of the Starving Mind
