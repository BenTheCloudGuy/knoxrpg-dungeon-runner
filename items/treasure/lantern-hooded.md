# Lantern, Hooded

- **Description:** A metal lantern with a sliding hood.
- **Use:** Casts Bright Light in a 30-foot radius and Dim Light for another 30 feet; burns 6 hours on a flask of oil. As a Bonus Action you can lower the hood to reduce it to Dim Light in a 5-foot radius.
- **Cost:** 5 GP
- **Weight:** 2 lb.
- **Source:** phb-2024, free-rules, phb, br
