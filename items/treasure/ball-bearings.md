# Ball Bearings

- **Description:** A pouch of tiny metal spheres.
- **Use:** As an action, spill these across a level 10-foot square. A creature that moves into the area makes a DC 10 Dexterity save or falls Prone.
- **Cost:** 1 GP
- **Weight:** 2 lb.
- **Source:** phb-2024, free-rules
