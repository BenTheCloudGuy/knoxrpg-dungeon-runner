# Manacles

- **Description:** A pair of iron wrist shackles.
- **Use:** Restrain a Small or Medium creature. Escaping requires a DC 20 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check; breaking them requires a DC 25 Strength check.
- **Cost:** 2 GP
- **Weight:** 6 lb.
