# Healer’s Kit

- **Description:** Bandages, salves, and splints in a leather pouch.
- **Use:** Has ten uses. As an action, expend one use to stabilize a creature at 0 Hit Points without needing a Wisdom (Medicine) check.
- **Cost:** 5 GP
- **Weight:** 3 lb.
- **Source:** phb-2024, free-rules, phb
