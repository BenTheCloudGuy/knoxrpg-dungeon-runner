# Smithing Tools

- **Description:** A single-use set of smithing supplies for quick forge work.
- **Use:** Expend this card to make one use of Smithing Tools: forge, reforge, or repair a metal item, or provide the smithing required by a room, recipe, or the Dwarven Forge.
- **Uses:** Each card is a single use.
- **Cost:** 2 GP
- **Source:** Custom - The Vault of the Starving Mind
