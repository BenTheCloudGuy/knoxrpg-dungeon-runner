# Poison, basic (vial)

- **Description:** A vial of basic poison.
- **Use:** As a Bonus Action, coat one weapon or up to three pieces of ammunition. A creature hit takes an extra 1d4 Poison damage. The coating stays potent for 1 minute.
- **Cost:** 100 gp
- **Weight:** -
- **Source:** phb, br
