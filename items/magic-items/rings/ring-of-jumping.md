# Ring of Jumping

![Ring of Jumping](../images/ring-of-jumping.png)

Ring, uncommon (requires attunement)
 
 
While wearing this ring, you can cast Jump from it, but can target only yourself when you do so.

Dungeon Master’s Guide, pg. 293
