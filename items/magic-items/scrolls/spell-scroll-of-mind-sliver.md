# Spell Scroll: Mind Sliver

![Spell Scroll: Mind Sliver](../images/spell-scroll.png)

Scroll, common

A Spell Scroll can be cast only if the spell is on your class's spell list. Casting from a scroll needs no material components, and the scroll crumbles to dust once the cast completes.

- **Level / School:** Cantrip, Enchantment
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** 1 round
- **Scroll Save DC / Attack:** DC 13 / +5

**Effect:** One creature you can see within range makes an Intelligence saving throw (DC 13). On a failed save, the target takes 1d6 Psychic damage, and it subtracts 1d4 from the next saving throw it makes before the end of your next turn.
