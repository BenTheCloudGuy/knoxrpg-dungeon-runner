# Spell Scroll: Scorching Ray

![Spell Scroll: Scorching Ray](../images/spell-scroll.png)

Scroll, uncommon

A Spell Scroll can be cast only if the spell is on your class's spell list. Casting from a scroll needs no material components, and the scroll crumbles to dust once the cast completes.

- **Level / School:** 2nd, Evocation
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Scroll Save DC / Attack:** DC 13 / +5

**Effect:** You create three rays of fire. Make a ranged spell attack (scroll +5) for each ray. You can aim the rays at one target or several. On a hit, a target takes 2d6 Fire damage.
