# Spell Scroll: Lesser Restoration

![Spell Scroll: Lesser Restoration](../images/spell-scroll.png)

Scroll, uncommon

A Spell Scroll can be cast only if the spell is on your class's spell list. Casting from a scroll needs no material components, and the scroll crumbles to dust once the cast completes.

- **Level / School:** 2nd, Abjuration
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Effect:** You touch a creature and end one of these conditions on it: Blinded, Deafened, Paralyzed, or Poisoned.
