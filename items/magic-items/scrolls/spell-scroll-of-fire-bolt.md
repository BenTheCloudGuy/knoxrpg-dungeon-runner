# Spell Scroll: Fire Bolt

![Spell Scroll: Fire Bolt](../images/spell-scroll.png)

Scroll, common

A Spell Scroll can be cast only if the spell is on your class's spell list. Casting from a scroll needs no material components, and the scroll crumbles to dust once the cast completes.

- **Level / School:** Cantrip, Evocation
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Scroll Save DC / Attack:** DC 13 / +5

**Effect:** Make a ranged spell attack (scroll +5) against one creature or object within range. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 Fire damage. A flammable object hit by this spell starts burning if it isn't being worn or carried.
