# Spell Scroll: Guidance

![Spell Scroll: Guidance](../images/spell-scroll.png)

Scroll, common

A Spell Scroll can be cast only if the spell is on your class's spell list. Casting from a scroll needs no material components, and the scroll crumbles to dust once the cast completes.

- **Level / School:** Cantrip, Divination
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

**Effect:** Touch a willing creature and choose one skill. Until the spell ends, the creature adds 1d4 to any ability check it makes using that skill.
