# Spell Scroll: See Invisibility

![Spell Scroll: See Invisibility](../images/spell-scroll.png)

Scroll, uncommon

A Spell Scroll can be cast only if the spell is on your class's spell list. Casting from a scroll needs no material components, and the scroll crumbles to dust once the cast completes.

- **Level / School:** 2nd, Divination
- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S (the scroll supplies the material component)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

**Effect:** For the duration, you see Invisible creatures and objects as if they were visible, and you can see into the Ethereal Plane. Ethereal creatures and objects appear ghostly.
