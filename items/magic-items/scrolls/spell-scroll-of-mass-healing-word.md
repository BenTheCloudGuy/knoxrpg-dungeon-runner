# Spell Scroll: Mass Healing Word

![Spell Scroll: Mass Healing Word](../images/spell-scroll.png)

Scroll, uncommon

A Spell Scroll can be cast only if the spell is on your class's spell list. Casting from a scroll needs no material components, and the scroll crumbles to dust once the cast completes.

- **Level / School:** 3rd, Abjuration
- **Casting Time:** Bonus Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Effect:** Up to six creatures of your choice that you can see within range each regain 2d4 plus your spellcasting ability modifier Hit Points.
