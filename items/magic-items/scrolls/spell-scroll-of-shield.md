# Spell Scroll: Shield

![Spell Scroll: Shield](../images/spell-scroll.png)

Scroll, common

A Spell Scroll can be cast only if the spell is on your class's spell list. Casting from a scroll needs no material components, and the scroll crumbles to dust once the cast completes.

- **Level / School:** 1st, Abjuration
- **Casting Time:** Reaction, taken when you are hit by an attack roll or targeted by the Magic Missile spell
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 round

**Effect:** You gain a plus 5 bonus to Armor Class until the start of your next turn, including against the triggering attack, and you take no damage from Magic Missile.
