# Three-Headed Snake Whip

Weapon (whip), rare (requires attunement)


You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

On a hit, this weapon deals an extra 1d4 + 1 Poison damage.

On a Critical Hit, the target must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or have the Poisoned condition. The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

Proficiency with a Whip allows you to add your Proficiency Bonus to the attack rolls you make with it.



This weapon has the following mastery property. To use this property, you must have a feature that lets you use it.


Slow. If you hit a creature with this weapon and deal damage to it, you can reduce its Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. If the creature is hit more than once by weapons that have this property, the Speed reduction doesn't exceed 10 feet.
