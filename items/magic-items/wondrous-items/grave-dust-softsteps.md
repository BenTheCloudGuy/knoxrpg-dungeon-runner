# Grave-Dust Softsteps

![Grave-Dust Softsteps](../images/grave-dust-softsteps.png)

Once per numbered room, when you make a Dexterity (Stealth) check while moving at half speed through rubble, loose stone, dust, or ruins, you can add +1 to the roll.
