# Bracers of Measured Draw

![Bracers of Measured Draw](../images/bracers-of-measured-draw.png)

When you hit with a nonmagical Longbow or Shortbow, add +1 to the damage roll; these bracers do not grant proficiency and do not affect attack rolls.
