# Bracers of Anchor Grip

![Bracers of Anchor Grip](../images/bracers-of-anchor-grip.png)

Once per numbered room, when you make a Strength or Dexterity saving throw or ability check to avoid being pushed, pulled, knocked Prone, disarmed, or made to fall, you can add +1 to the roll.
