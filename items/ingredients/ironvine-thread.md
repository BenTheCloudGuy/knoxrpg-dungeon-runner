# Ironvine Thread

- **Appearance:** Thin dark vine threaded with metallic fibers.
- **Simple Test:** Touch both ends to the iron stirring rod: the thread pulls taut.
- **Handling:** Keep coiled loosely when not in use. It will snap taut and tangle if left touching iron for long.
- **Found:** Alchemist Chamber
