# Thunderhead Lichen

- **Appearance:** Crinkled yellow lichen that smells of ozone after rain.
- **Simple Test:** Tap it with the glass rod: it snaps once with a tiny spark.
- **Handling:** Use the glass rod, not a metal one, to test it. It only has the one spark in it; a wasted test on a sample will not repeat.
- **Found:** Alchemist Chamber
