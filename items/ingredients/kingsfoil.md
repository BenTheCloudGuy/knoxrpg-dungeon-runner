# Kingsfoil

- **Appearance:** Dry green leaves with silver undersides and a pine-and-mint aroma when crushed.
- **Simple Test:** Crush one leaf in a dry palm: nearby dust lifts toward the scent.
- **Handling:** Handle dry. Keep leaves flat and uncrushed until testing, or the aroma fades before it can be judged.
- **Found:** Alchemist Chamber
