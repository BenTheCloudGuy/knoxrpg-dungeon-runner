# Troll Blood

- **Appearance:** Thick dark-red paste with a copper smell.
- **Simple Test:** Cut a groove with a spoon: the groove slowly closes.
- **Handling:** Store in a sealed jar. Left open on the bench, it will slowly heal over any container it is left in, sealing lids shut.
- **Found:** Alchemist Chamber
