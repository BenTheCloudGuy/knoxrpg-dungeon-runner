# Black Dragon Scale Powder

- **Appearance:** Dull black-green flakes with a sharp vinegar bite.
- **Simple Test:** Touch with a wet iron probe: tiny pits appear after several seconds.
- **Handling:** Wipe any probe used for testing before it touches skin or other samples. The bite in the air is sharp enough to sting the eyes up close.
- **Found:** Alchemist Chamber
