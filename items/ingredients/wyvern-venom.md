# Wyvern Venom

- **Appearance:** Clear yellow oily fluid with a bitter medicinal smell.
- **Simple Test:** Place one drop on fresh meat: the edges gray.
- **Handling:** Do not taste or touch to open skin. Use the dropper provided and re-cork the vial immediately after testing.
- **Found:** Alchemist Chamber
