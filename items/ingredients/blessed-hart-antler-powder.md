# Blessed Hart Antler Powder

- **Appearance:** Fine white crystals with a clean mineral scent.
- **Simple Test:** Add to polluted water: the water visibly clears.
- **Handling:** Keep dry and sealed. A damp sample clumps and will not disperse cleanly when tested.
- **Found:** Alchemist Chamber
