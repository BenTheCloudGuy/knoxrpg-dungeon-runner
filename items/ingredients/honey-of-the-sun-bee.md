# Honey of the Sun Bee

- **Appearance:** Thick amber syrup with floral and orange-peel notes. It feels warm on the tongue.
- **Simple Test:** Let a drop fall on a cool spoon: it crawls uphill toward warmth.
- **Handling:** Keep the jar upright and capped. It draws insects quickly if left open on the bench.
- **Found:** Alchemist Chamber
