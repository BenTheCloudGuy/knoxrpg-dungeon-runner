# Charcoal of Salamander Bone

- **Appearance:** Black powder with glowing red flecks and a cold-fireplace smell.
- **Simple Test:** Hold it near flame: red flecks brighten without burning.
- **Handling:** Keep the sample dry and away from ash pits. It will not ignite on its own, but it clumps if it gets damp.
- **Found:** Alchemist Chamber
