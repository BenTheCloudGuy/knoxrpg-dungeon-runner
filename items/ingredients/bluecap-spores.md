# Bluecap Spores

- **Appearance:** Powdery blue spores with a sweet bread smell.
- **Simple Test:** Breathe across them: they puff into a blue ring and settle.
- **Handling:** Do not inhale the puff directly. Test with the container held at arm's length and breathe across, not into, the spores.
- **Found:** Alchemist Chamber
