# Nightshade Berries

- **Appearance:** Dried violet-black berries with bitter almond and herb notes.
- **Simple Test:** Crush one: fingers stain purple.
- **Handling:** Wash hands after handling. Do not taste; the bitter almond smell alone is the warning.
- **Found:** Alchemist Chamber
