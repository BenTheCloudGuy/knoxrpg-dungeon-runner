# Glassroot Sap

- **Appearance:** Clear, stringy sap with a faint ringing tone when stretched.
- **Simple Test:** Pull a thread between two spoons: it hardens into a glass filament.
- **Handling:** Once pulled into a filament it is brittle and sharp-edged. Handle the hardened thread carefully to avoid cuts.
- **Found:** Alchemist Chamber
