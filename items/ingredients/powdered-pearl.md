# Powdered Pearl

- **Appearance:** Fine ivory powder, impossibly smooth and odorless. In water it makes faint silver swirls.
- **Simple Test:** Stir a pinch into water: silver ribbons spiral before settling.
- **Handling:** Keep the vial capped and dry. A stray draft or wet spoon ruins a sample before it can be tested.
- **Found:** Alchemist Chamber (multiple), Treasure Goblin, treasure chests
