# Cinder Reed

- **Appearance:** Hollow red-brown reed with a peppery smoke scent.
- **Simple Test:** Blow through it: the far end exhales a brief ember glow.
- **Handling:** Keep away from open flame and dry tinder. The glow is brief and harmless, but repeated blowing wears the reed out.
- **Found:** Alchemist Chamber
