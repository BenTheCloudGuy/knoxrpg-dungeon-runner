# Grave Moss

- **Appearance:** Gray-green fibrous moss smelling of wet earth and mushrooms.
- **Simple Test:** Expose it to bright light: it contracts.
- **Handling:** Store in shade or a covered jar. Direct sunlight or a held torch will shrivel a sample before it can be judged fairly.
- **Found:** Alchemist Chamber
