# Moonwater

- **Appearance:** Clear liquid smelling of rain on stone. Shaking makes blue-white sparks.
- **Simple Test:** Shake in a sealed vial: sparks vanish when the vial stops moving.
- **Handling:** Keep the vial sealed between tests. Once opened to air for long, the sparking fades and does not return.
- **Found:** Alchemist Chamber
