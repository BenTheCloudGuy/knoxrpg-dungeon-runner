# Fire Beetle Gland

- **Appearance:** A small glistening lobed sac, dark orange-red and shot through with fine veining, wrapped in a tough translucent membrane. It smells of hot metal and burnt cinnamon. The vial feels warm to the touch.
- **Simple Test:** Squeeze it gently near a flame: the oily fluid inside glows through the membrane but does not ignite.
- **Handling:** Keep away from open flame until testing. The vial runs warm on its own; do not seal it inside a pocket for long stretches.
- **Found:** Alchemist Chamber
