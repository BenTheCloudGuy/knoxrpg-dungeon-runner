# Frostsalt

- **Appearance:** Translucent blue grains that sting the nose like cold air.
- **Simple Test:** Sprinkle on warm water: a skin of ice forms in seconds.
- **Handling:** Keep the container sealed and away from bare skin. Spilled grains will frost over whatever they land on.
- **Found:** Alchemist Chamber
