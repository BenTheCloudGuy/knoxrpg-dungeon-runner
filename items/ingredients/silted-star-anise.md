# Silted Star Anise

- **Appearance:** Rust-colored star pods with licorice and river-mud scent.
- **Simple Test:** Drop one in water: it points toward the nearest metal object.
- **Handling:** Keep away from stacks of coins or iron tools during testing, or the pointer gives a misleading result.
- **Found:** Alchemist Chamber
