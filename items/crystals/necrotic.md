# Necrotic

- **School:** Necromancy
- **Boon:** Life Siphon
- **Power:** **Life Siphon:** Whenever you hit a creature with any martial weapon while holding this Crystal - you deal an extra 1d4 necrotic damage and as a bonus action you can recover HP that is 1/2 the necrotic damage the creature takes (minimum 1 HP)
