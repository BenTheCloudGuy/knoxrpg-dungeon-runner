# Red

- **School:** Evocation
- **Boon:** Crystal Burst
- **Power:** **Crystal Burst:** As an action, choose a point within 60 feet. Creatures in a 10-foot-radius sphere make a DC 13 Dexterity save, taking 2d6 fire damage on a failure, or half as much damage on a success. The bearer chooses which creatures are affected, so new players do not need to worry about friendly fire.<br /><br />Can only be used 1 time per game hour.
