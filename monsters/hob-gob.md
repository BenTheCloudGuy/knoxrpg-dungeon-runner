# Hob Gob, the Goblin King (Boss)

*Medium Humanoid (Goblinoid), Lawful Evil*

**Armor Class** 18 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 178 (21d8 + 84)
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT     | WIS     | CHA     |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- |
| 18 (+4) | 14 (+2) | 18 (+4) | 13 (+1) | 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) |

**Saving Throws** Str +7, Con +7, Wis +4, Cha +6
**Skills** Athletics +7, Intimidation +6, Perception +4, Stealth +5
**Condition Immunities** frightened
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14
**Languages** Common, Goblin
**Challenge** 8 (3,900 XP), **Proficiency Bonus** +3

### Traits

***Magic Resistance.*** Hob Gob has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Regeneration.*** Hob Gob regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of his turns if he has at least 1 Hit Point. If Hob Gob takes Acid or Fire damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of his next turn. Hob Gob dies only if he starts his turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.

### Spellcasting

Hob Gob is a level 5 Druid spellcaster. Wisdom is his spellcasting ability. His spell save DC is 12, and he has +4 to hit with spell attacks. He has the following Druid spells prepared:

- ***Cantrips, at will:***
  - *Druidcraft:* 30 ft.; no save or attack; creates a tiny harmless sensory effect, predicts the weather, makes a flower bloom, or lights or snuffs a small flame; instantaneous or up to 1 hour; no concentration.
  - *Produce Flame:* self, with a 30 ft. ranged spell attack at +4; 2d8 Fire damage on a hit, or sheds bright light in a 10 ft. radius and dim light for 10 ft. more; 10 minutes; no concentration.
  - *Thorn Whip:* 30 ft. melee spell attack at +4; 2d6 Piercing damage on a hit, and a Large or smaller target can be pulled up to 10 ft. closer; instantaneous; no concentration.
- ***1st level, 4 slots:***
  - *Cure Wounds:* touch; no save or attack; one creature regains 2d8 + 1 Hit Points, plus 2d8 per slot level above 1st; instantaneous; no concentration.
  - *Entangle:* 90 ft., 20 ft. square; Strength save DC 12 or be Restrained by grasping plants, with a new save after using an action to break free; area is Difficult Terrain; concentration, up to 1 minute.
  - *Faerie Fire:* 60 ft., 20 ft. cube; Dexterity save DC 12 or be outlined in light, granting Advantage on attack rolls against the target and negating benefits from being Invisible; concentration, up to 1 minute.
  - *Thunderwave:* self, 15 ft. cube; Constitution save DC 12; 2d8 Thunder damage and pushed 10 ft. on a failed save, half damage and no push on a success; instantaneous; no concentration.
- ***2nd level, 3 slots:***
  - *Lesser Restoration:* touch; no save or attack; ends one condition on a creature, Blinded, Deafened, Paralyzed, or Poisoned; instantaneous; no concentration.
  - *Moonbeam:* 120 ft., 5 ft. radius, 40 ft. high cylinder; Constitution save DC 12; 2d10 Radiant damage when a creature enters the beam for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, half damage on a success; concentration, up to 1 minute.
  - *Spike Growth:* 150 ft., 20 ft. radius; no save; ground becomes Difficult Terrain, and a creature takes 2d4 Piercing damage for every 5 ft. it moves through the area; concentration, up to 10 minutes.
- ***3rd level, 2 slots:***
  - *Call Lightning:* 120 ft., storm cloud above a point Hob Gob can see; Dexterity save DC 12; 3d10 Lightning damage in a 5 ft. radius under the bolt, half damage on a success, and Hob Gob can call another bolt with his action while the spell lasts; concentration, up to 10 minutes.
  - *Plant Growth:* 150 ft., 100 ft. radius; no save; normal plants become thick and overgrown, and each 1 ft. of movement through the area costs 4 ft. of movement; instantaneous; no concentration.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** Hob Gob makes three attacks: one Jagged Bite attack and two Hooked Cleaver attacks.

***Jagged Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Piercing damage.

***Hooked Cleaver.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Slashing damage.
