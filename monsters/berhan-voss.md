| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------ | ------- | ------ |
| 18 (+4) | 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 6 (-2) | 10 (+0) | 6 (-2) |

**Skills** Perception +3
**Damage Resistances** acid, poison
**Condition Immunities** poisoned
