# Berhan Voss, the Alchemist (Mutant)

*Medium Aberration (Mutated Humanoid), Unaligned*

**Armor Class** 16 (natural armor, scarred and acid-hardened flesh)
**Hit Points** 76 (9d8 + 36)
**Speed** 30 ft., climb 20 ft. (can move on walls and ceilings; the mutation has no sense of "up")

| STR     | DEX    | CON     | INT    | WIS    | CHA    |
| ------- | ------ | ------- | ------ | ------ | ------ |
| 20 (+5) | 10 (0) | 18 (+4) | 6 (-2) | 8 (-1) | 6 (-2) |

**Saving Throws** Str +8
**Skills** Perception +3
**Damage Resistances** acid
**Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13
**Languages** understands Common but can't speak; grunts and wet, wrong-sounding vowels
**Challenge** 5 (1,800 XP), **Proficiency Bonus** +3

### Traits

***Berserk.*** Whenever Berhan starts his turn with 40 Hit Points or fewer, roll a d6. On a 6, Berhan goes berserk. On each of his turns while berserk, Berhan attacks the nearest creature he can see. If no creature is near enough to move to and attack, Berhan attacks an object, with preference for an object smaller than himself. Once Berhan goes berserk, he continues to do so until he is destroyed or regains all his Hit Points.

Berhan's creator, if within 60 feet of Berhan, can try to calm him by speaking firmly and persuasively. Berhan must be able to hear his creator, who must take an action to make a DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) check. On a successful check, Berhan ceases being berserk. If Berhan takes damage while still at 40 Hit Points or fewer, he might go berserk again.

***Aversion of Fire.*** If Berhan takes Fire damage, he has Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks until the end of his next turn.

***Immutable Form.*** Berhan is immune to any spell or other effect that would alter his form.

***Lightning Absorption.*** Whenever Berhan is subjected to Lightning damage, he takes no damage and instead regains a number of Hit Points equal to the Lightning damage dealt.

***Magic Resistance.*** Berhan has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** Berhan makes two Slam attacks.

***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage.
