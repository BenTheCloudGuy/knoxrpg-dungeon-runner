# Lizard Shaman

*Medium Humanoid (Lizardfolk), Neutral*

**Armor Class** 15 (natural armor, shield)
**Hit Points** 22 (4d8 + 4)
**Speed** 30 ft., swim 30 ft.

| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT    | WIS     | CHA    |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------ | ------- | ------ |
| 15 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 13 (+1) | 7 (-2) | 16 (+3) | 7 (-2) |

**Skills** Perception +5, Stealth +4, Survival +7
**Senses** passive Perception 15
**Languages** Draconic
**Challenge** 1/2 (100 XP), **Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Traits

***Hold Breath.*** The lizardfolk can hold its breath for 15 minutes.

### Spellcasting

The lizardfolk is a level 4 Druid spellcaster built on the official Lizardfolk chassis. Wisdom is its spellcasting ability. Its spell save DC is 13, and it has +5 to hit with spell attacks. It has 4 first-level spell slots and 3 second-level spell slots, and it has the following Druid spells prepared:

- ***Cantrips, at will:***
  - *Druidcraft:* 30 ft.; no save or attack; creates a tiny harmless sensory effect, predicts the weather, makes a flower bloom, or lights or snuffs a small flame; instantaneous or up to 1 hour; no concentration.
  - *Guidance:* touch; no save or attack; choose one skill, and one willing creature adds 1d4 to ability checks using that skill; concentration, up to 1 minute.
  - *Produce Flame:* self, with a 30 ft. ranged spell attack at +5; 1d8 Fire damage on a hit, or sheds bright light in a 10 ft. radius and dim light for 10 ft. more; 10 minutes; no concentration.
- ***1st level, 4 slots:***
  - *Cure Wounds:* touch; no save or attack; one creature regains 2d8 + 3 Hit Points, plus 2d8 per slot level above 1st; instantaneous; no concentration.
  - *Entangle:* 90 ft., 20 ft. square; Strength save DC 13 or be Restrained by grasping plants, with a new save after using an action to break free; area is Difficult Terrain; concentration, up to 1 minute.
  - *Faerie Fire:* 60 ft., 20 ft. cube; Dexterity save DC 13 or be outlined in light, granting Advantage on attack rolls against the target and negating benefits from being Invisible; concentration, up to 1 minute.
  - *Healing Word:* 60 ft.; no save or attack; one creature the lizardfolk can see regains 2d4 + 3 Hit Points; instantaneous; no concentration.
- ***2nd level, 3 slots:***
  - *Lesser Restoration:* touch; no save or attack; ends one condition on a creature, Blinded, Deafened, Paralyzed, or Poisoned; instantaneous; no concentration.
  - *Moonbeam:* 120 ft., 5 ft. radius, 40 ft. high cylinder; Constitution save DC 13; 2d10 Radiant damage when a creature enters the beam for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, half damage on a success; concentration, up to 1 minute.
  - *Spike Growth:* 150 ft., 20 ft. radius; no save; ground becomes Difficult Terrain, and a creature takes 2d4 Piercing damage for every 5 feet it moves through the area; concentration, up to 10 minutes.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The lizardfolk makes two melee attacks, each one with a different weapon.

***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage.

***Heavy Club.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Bludgeoning damage.

***Javelin.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage.

***Spiked Shield.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage.
