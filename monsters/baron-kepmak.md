# Baron Kepmak

*Medium Humanoid (Lizardfolk), Chaotic Evil*

**Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 78 (12d8 + 24)
**Speed** 30 ft., swim 30 ft.

| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT     | WIS     | CHA     |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- |
| 17 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 11 (+0) | 11 (+0) | 15 (+2) |

**Saving Throws** Con +4, Wis +2
**Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +5, Survival +4
**Senses** passive Perception 14
**Languages** Abyssal, Draconic
**Challenge** 4 (1,100 XP), **Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Traits

***Hold Breath.*** Kepmak can hold his breath for 15 minutes.

***Skewer.*** Once on each of Kepmak's turns, when he makes a melee attack with his Trident and hits a creature, the target takes an extra 10 (3d6) Piercing damage, and Kepmak gains Temporary Hit Points equal to the extra damage dealt.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** Kepmak makes two attacks: one Bite attack and one Claws or Trident attack.

***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage.

***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Slashing damage.

***Trident.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage, or 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage if used with two hands to make a melee attack.
