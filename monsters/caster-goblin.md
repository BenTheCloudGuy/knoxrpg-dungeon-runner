# Caster Goblin (x1)

*Small Fey (Goblinoid), Chaotic Neutral*

**Armor Class** 15 (leather armor, shield)
**Hit Points** 10 (3d6)
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR    | DEX     | CON     | INT     | WIS    | CHA    |
| ------ | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------ | ------ |
| 8 (-1) | 15 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 8 (-1) | 14 (+2) |

**Skills** Stealth +6
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 9
**Languages** Common, Goblin
**Challenge** 1/4 (50 XP), **Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Traits

None.

### Spellcasting

The goblin is a level 3 Sorcerer spellcaster. Charisma is its spellcasting ability. Its spell save DC is 12, and it has +4 to hit with spell attacks. It has 4 first-level spell slots and 2 second-level spell slots, and it has the following Sorcerer spells prepared:

- ***Cantrips, at will:***
  - *Fire Bolt:* 120 ft. ranged spell attack at +4; 1d10 Fire damage on a hit; instantaneous; no concentration.
  - *Ray of Frost:* 60 ft. ranged spell attack at +4; 1d8 Cold damage on a hit, and the target's Speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of the goblin's next turn; instantaneous; no concentration.
  - *Minor Illusion:* 30 ft.; no attack; creates one sound or one image of an object; a creature that examines it can identify it as an illusion with a successful DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check; 1 minute; no concentration.
  - *Prestidigitation:* 10 ft.; no save or attack; creates a harmless sensory effect, lights or snuffs a small flame, cleans or soils a small object, chills, warms, or flavors nonliving material, or makes a small mark; up to 1 hour depending on the effect; no concentration.
- ***1st level, 4 slots:***
  - *Magic Missile:* 120 ft.; no attack roll or save; three darts each deal 1d4 + 1 Force damage to creatures the goblin can see; instantaneous; no concentration.
  - *Shield:* self, reaction when hit by an attack or targeted by *Magic Missile*; no save or attack; +5 AC until the start of the goblin's next turn, including against the triggering attack, and it blocks *Magic Missile*; 1 round; no concentration.
  - *Thunderwave:* self, 15 ft. cube; Constitution save DC 12; 2d8 Thunder damage and pushed 10 feet on a failed save, half damage and no push on a success; instantaneous; no concentration.
- ***2nd level, 2 slots:***
  - *Scorching Ray:* 120 ft.; three ranged spell attacks at +4; each ray deals 2d6 Fire damage on a hit; instantaneous; no concentration.
  - *Misty Step:* self; no save or attack; the goblin teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space it can see; instantaneous; no concentration.
  - *Invisibility:* touch; no save or attack; one creature has the Invisible condition until the spell ends, and the spell ends early for a target that attacks, deals damage, or casts a spell; concentration, up to 1 hour.

### Actions

***Quarterstaff.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +1, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 2 (1d6 - 1) Bludgeoning damage, or 3 (1d8 - 1) Bludgeoning damage if used with two hands.

***Scimitar.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Slashing damage, plus 2 (1d4) Slashing damage if the attack roll had Advantage.

***Shortbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:* +4, range 80/320 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage, plus 2 (1d4) Piercing damage if the attack roll had Advantage.

### Bonus Actions

***Nimble Escape.*** The goblin takes the Disengage or Hide action.
