# Green Slaad

*Large Aberration, Chaotic Neutral*

**Armor Class** 16 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 127 (15d10 + 45)
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT      | WIS     | CHA     |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | -------- | ------- | ------- |
| 18 (+4) | 15 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 11 (+0) | 8 (-1) | 12 (+1) |

**Skills** Arcana +3, Perception +2
**Damage Resistances** Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Thunder
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12
**Languages** Slaad, telepathy 60 ft.
**Challenge** 8 (3,900 XP), **Proficiency Bonus** +3

### Traits

***Magic Resistance.*** The slaad has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Regeneration.*** The slaad regains 10 Hit Points at the start of each of its turns if it has at least 1 Hit Point. If the slaad takes Acid or Fire damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the slaad's next turn. The slaad dies only if it starts its turn with 0 Hit Points and doesn't regenerate.

***Shapechanger.*** The slaad can use its action to polymorph into a Small or Medium Humanoid, or back into its true form. Its statistics, other than its size, are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed. It reverts to its true form if it dies.

***Innate Spellcasting.*** The slaad's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma. Its spell save DC is 12, and it has +4 to hit with spell attacks. It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:

- ***At will:***
  - *Detect Magic:* self, 30 ft. aura; no save or attack; senses magic and can reveal its school with an action; concentration, up to 10 minutes.
  - *Detect Thoughts:* self, 30 ft.; Wisdom save DC 12 only if the slaad probes deeper; reads surface thoughts and can probe for deeper thoughts on a failed save; concentration, up to 1 minute.
  - *Mage Hand:* 30 ft.; no save or attack; creates a spectral hand that can manipulate objects and carry up to 10 pounds; 1 minute; no concentration.
- ***2/day each:***
  - *Fear:* self, 30 ft. cone; Wisdom save DC 12 or drop held objects and have the Frightened condition, with repeat saves if the target ends its turn out of line of sight; concentration, up to 1 minute.
  - *Invisibility:* self; no save or attack; the slaad has the Invisible condition until it attacks, deals damage, casts a spell, or the spell ends; concentration, up to 1 hour.
- ***1/day:***
  - *Fireball:* 150 ft., 20 ft. radius; Dexterity save DC 12; 8d6 Fire damage on a failed save, half damage on a success; instantaneous; no concentration.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The slaad makes three attacks: one Bite attack and two Claws or Staff attacks.

***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +7, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Piercing damage.

***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +7, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Slashing damage.

***Staff.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +7, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Bludgeoning damage.

***Hurl Flame.*** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +4, range 60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (3d6) Fire damage. The fire ignites flammable objects that aren't being worn or carried.
