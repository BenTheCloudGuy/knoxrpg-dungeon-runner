# Sahuagin Baron

*Large Humanoid (Sahuagin), Lawful Evil*

**Armor Class** 16 (breastplate)
**Hit Points** 76 (9d10 + 27)
**Speed** 30 ft., swim 50 ft.

| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT     | WIS     | CHA     |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- |
| 19 (+4) | 15 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 13 (+1) | 17 (+3) |

**Saving Throws** Dex +5, Con +6, Int +5, Wis +4
**Skills** Perception +7
**Senses** darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 17
**Languages** Sahuagin
**Challenge** 5 (1,800 XP), **Proficiency Bonus** +3

### Traits

***Blood Frenzy.*** The sahuagin has Advantage on melee attack rolls against any creature that doesn't have all its Hit Points.

***Limited Amphibiousness.*** The sahuagin can breathe air and water, but it needs to be submerged at least once every 4 hours to avoid suffocating.

***Shark Telepathy.*** The sahuagin can magically command any shark within 120 feet of itself, using a limited telepathy.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The sahuagin makes three attacks: one Bite attack and two Claws or Trident attacks.

***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +7, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (2d4 + 4) Piercing damage.

***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +7, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Slashing damage.

***Trident.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +7, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Piercing damage, or 13 (2d8 + 4) Piercing damage if used with two hands to make a melee attack.
