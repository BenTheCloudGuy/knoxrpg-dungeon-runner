# Goblin Artificer (x1, Goblin Camp)

*Small Fey (Goblinoid), Chaotic Neutral*

**Armor Class** 13 (leather armor)
**Hit Points** 10 (3d6)
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR    | DEX     | CON     | INT     | WIS    | CHA    |
| ------ | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------ | ------ |
| 8 (-1) | 15 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 8 (-1) | 8 (-1) |

**Skills** Stealth +6
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 9
**Languages** Common, Goblin
**Challenge** 1/4 (50 XP), **Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Traits

None.

### Actions

***Pistol.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:* +4, range 30/90 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d10 + 2) Piercing damage, plus 2 (1d4) Piercing damage if the attack roll had Advantage.

***Goblin Artificer's Scoped Musket.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:* +5, range 40/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (1d12 + 3) Piercing damage. This magic musket gives a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it, and its scope ignores the Disadvantage normally imposed for attacking at long range.

***Dagger.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage, plus 2 (1d4) Piercing damage if the attack roll had Advantage.

### Bonus Actions

***Nimble Escape.*** The goblin takes the Disengage or Hide action.
