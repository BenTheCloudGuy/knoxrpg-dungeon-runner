# Vos'sykriss, the Serpentfolk

*Medium Monstrosity (Serpentfolk), typically Chaotic Evil*

**Armor Class** 15
**Hit Points** 85 (10d8 + 40)
**Speed** 30 ft., climb 20 ft.

| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT     | WIS     | CHA     |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- |
| 16 (+3) | 16 (+3) | 18 (+4) | 11 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 15 (+2) |

**Saving Throws** Dex +6, Con +7, Wis +5
**Skills** Perception +5, Stealth +6
**Damage Resistances** poison
**Condition Immunities** poisoned
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 15
**Languages** Common, Draconic
**Challenge** 5 (1,800 XP), **Proficiency Bonus** +3

### Traits

***Coiled Vigilance.*** Vos'sykriss has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks and on saving throws against being Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Stunned, or knocked Unconscious.

***Serpentine Awareness.*** Vos'sykriss can't be surprised while it is conscious.

***Weaving Coils.*** Attack rolls made as Opportunity Attacks against Vos'sykriss have Disadvantage.

***Invisible Doubles.*** Vos'sykriss is accompanied by two identical serpentfolk duplicates. Each duplicate uses this same stat block and has the Invisible condition until it attacks or is revealed by See Invisibility or another effect that reveals invisible creatures. The duplicates can't use Petrifying Gaze, only the true Vos'sykriss can petrify. A destroyed duplicate doesn't end the Invisible condition on the other duplicate or reveal the true Vos'sykriss. Identifying the real Vos'sykriss, either as the only serpentfolk whose gaze can petrify or through See Invisibility or an effect that reveals invisible creatures in an area, is part of the encounter puzzle.

***Petrifying Gaze.*** When a creature that can see Vos'sykriss's eyes starts its turn within 30 feet of it, Vos'sykriss can force that creature to make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw if Vos'sykriss isn't Incapacitated and can see the creature. On a failed save, the creature has the Restrained condition as it begins to turn to stone. At the end of its next turn, it repeats the save, ending the effect on a success and gaining the Petrified condition on a failure. A creature can avert its eyes at the start of its turn to avoid the save. If it does, it can't see Vos'sykriss until the start of its next turn.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** Vos'sykriss makes two attacks, choosing from Scimitar and Bite.

***Scimitar.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d10 + 3) Slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) Poison damage.

***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage.

***Constrict.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +6, reach 10 ft., one Medium or smaller creature. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14). Until the grapple ends, the target has the Restrained condition, and Vos'sykriss can't constrict another target.
