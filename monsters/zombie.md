# Zombie

*Medium Undead, Neutral Evil*

**Armor Class** 8
**Hit Points** 15 (2d8 + 6)
**Speed** 20 ft.

| STR     | DEX    | CON     | INT    | WIS    | CHA    |
| ------- | ------ | ------- | ------ | ------ | ------ |
| 13 (+1) | 6 (-2) | 16 (+3) | 3 (-4) | 6 (-2) | 5 (-3) |

**Saving Throws** Wis +0
**Damage Immunities** Poison
**Condition Immunities** Poisoned
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 8
**Languages** understands the languages it knew in life but can't speak
**Challenge** 1/4 (50 XP), **Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Traits

***Undead Fortitude.*** If damage reduces the zombie to 0 Hit Points, it makes a Constitution saving throw with a DC of 5 plus the damage taken, unless the damage is Radiant or from a Critical Hit. On a successful save, the zombie drops to 1 Hit Point instead.

### Actions

***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) Bludgeoning damage.
