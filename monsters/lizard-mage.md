# Lizard Mage

*Medium Humanoid (Lizardfolk), Neutral*

**Armor Class** 15 (natural armor, shield)
**Hit Points** 22 (4d8 + 4)
**Speed** 30 ft., swim 30 ft.

| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT     | WIS     | CHA    |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------ |
| 15 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 13 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 7 (-2) |

**Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +4, Survival +5
**Senses** passive Perception 13
**Languages** Draconic
**Challenge** 1/2 (100 XP), **Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Traits

***Hold Breath.*** The lizardfolk can hold its breath for 15 minutes.

### Spellcasting

The lizardfolk is a level 4 Wizard spellcaster built on the official Lizardfolk chassis. Intelligence is its spellcasting ability. Its spell save DC is 13, and it has +5 to hit with spell attacks. It has 4 first-level spell slots and 3 second-level spell slots, and it has the following Wizard spells prepared:

- ***Cantrips, at will:***
  - *Fire Bolt:* 120 ft. ranged spell attack at +5; 1d10 Fire damage on a hit; instantaneous; no concentration.
  - *Ray of Frost:* 60 ft. ranged spell attack at +5; 1d8 Cold damage on a hit, and the target's Speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of the lizardfolk's next turn; instantaneous; no concentration.
  - *Mage Hand:* 30 ft.; no save or attack; creates a spectral hand that can manipulate objects and carry up to 10 pounds; 1 minute; no concentration.
  - *Minor Illusion:* 30 ft.; no attack; creates one sound or one image of an object, and a creature that examines it can identify it as an illusion with a successful DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check; 1 minute; no concentration.
- ***1st level, 4 slots:***
  - *Grease:* 60 ft., 10 ft. square; Dexterity save DC 13 or fall Prone when the grease appears and when a creature enters or ends its turn in the area; area is Difficult Terrain; 1 minute; no concentration.
  - *Magic Missile:* 120 ft.; no attack roll or save; three darts each deal 1d4 + 1 Force damage to creatures the lizardfolk can see; instantaneous; no concentration.
  - *Shield:* self, reaction when hit by an attack or targeted by *Magic Missile*; no save or attack; +5 AC until the start of the lizardfolk's next turn, including against the triggering attack, and it blocks *Magic Missile*; 1 round; no concentration.
  - *Thunderwave:* self, 15 ft. cube; Constitution save DC 13; 2d8 Thunder damage and pushed 10 feet on a failed save, half damage and no push on a success; instantaneous; no concentration.
- ***2nd level, 3 slots:***
  - *Misty Step:* self; no save or attack; the lizardfolk teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space it can see; instantaneous; no concentration.
  - *Scorching Ray:* 120 ft.; three ranged spell attacks at +5; each ray deals 2d6 Fire damage on a hit; instantaneous; no concentration.
  - *Web:* 60 ft., 20 ft. cube; Dexterity save DC 13 or be Restrained by webs, with Strength checks against DC 13 to escape; the area is Difficult Terrain and lightly obscured; concentration, up to 1 hour.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The lizardfolk makes two melee attacks, each one with a different weapon.

***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage.

***Heavy Club.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Bludgeoning damage.

***Javelin.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage.

***Spiked Shield.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage.
