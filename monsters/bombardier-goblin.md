# Bombardier Goblin (x1)

*Small Fey (Goblinoid), Chaotic Neutral*

**Armor Class** 18 (plate armor)
**Hit Points** 21 (6d6)
**Speed** 20 ft.

| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT     | WIS    | CHA     |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------ | ------- |
| 10 (+0) | 15 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 8 (-1) | 10 (+0) |

**Skills** Stealth +6
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 9
**Languages** Common, Goblin
**Challenge** 1 (200 XP), **Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Traits

None.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The goblin makes two Shortsword attacks.

***Gob Stopper (10).*** The goblin lights and throws a Gob Stopper up to 30 feet. It explodes on impact. Each creature in range makes a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw. A creature within 10 feet takes 7 (2d6) Fire damage on a failed save. A creature more than 10 feet but within 15 feet takes 3 (1d6) Fire damage on a failed save. A creature takes half as much damage on a successful save.

***Shortsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage, plus 2 (1d4) Piercing damage if the attack roll had Advantage.

***Dagger.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage, plus 2 (1d4) Piercing damage if the attack roll had Advantage.

### Bonus Actions

***Nimble Escape.*** The goblin takes the Disengage or Hide action.

### Reactions

***Redirect Attack.*** When a creature the goblin can see targets it with an attack, the goblin chooses another goblin within 5 feet of it. The two goblins swap places, and the chosen goblin becomes the target instead.
