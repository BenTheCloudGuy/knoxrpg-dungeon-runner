# Shaman Goblin / Medicine Man (Healer) (x1, Goblin Camp)

*Small Fey (Goblinoid), Chaotic Neutral*

**Armor Class** 15 (leather armor, shield)
**Hit Points** 10 (3d6)
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR    | DEX     | CON     | INT     | WIS    | CHA    |
| ------ | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------ | ------ |
| 8 (-1) | 15 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 8 (-1) |

**Skills** Stealth +6
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12
**Languages** Common, Goblin
**Challenge** 1/4 (50 XP), **Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Traits

None.

### Spellcasting

The goblin is a level 2 Cleric spellcaster. Wisdom is its spellcasting ability. Its spell save DC is 12, and it has +4 to hit with spell attacks. It has 3 first-level spell slots and no second-level spell slots, and it has the following Cleric spells prepared:

- ***Cantrips, at will:***
  - *Sacred Flame:* 60 ft.; Dexterity save DC 12; 1d8 Radiant damage on a failed save, with no benefit from cover for the save; instantaneous; no concentration.
  - *Guidance:* touch; no save or attack; the target chooses one skill and adds 1d4 to ability checks using that skill; concentration, up to 1 minute.
  - *Thaumaturgy:* 30 ft.; no save or attack; creates a harmless divine sign such as a louder voice, a tremor, a sound, or a minor visual effect; up to 1 minute; no concentration.
- ***1st level, 3 slots:***
  - *Cure Wounds:* touch; no save or attack; one creature regains 2d8 + 2 Hit Points; instantaneous; no concentration.
  - *Bless:* 30 ft.; no save or attack; up to three creatures add 1d4 to attack rolls and saving throws; concentration, up to 1 minute.
  - *Healing Word:* 60 ft.; no save or attack; one creature the goblin can see regains 2d4 + 2 Hit Points; instantaneous; no concentration.
  - *Sanctuary:* 30 ft.; Wisdom save DC 12 when a creature attacks the warded target; on a failed save, the attacker must choose a new target or lose the attack; 1 minute; no concentration, and the spell ends early if the warded target attacks, deals damage, or casts a spell that affects an enemy.

### Actions

***Scimitar.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Slashing damage, plus 2 (1d4) Slashing damage if the attack roll had Advantage.

***Shortbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:* +4, range 80/320 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage, plus 2 (1d4) Piercing damage if the attack roll had Advantage.

### Bonus Actions

***Nimble Escape.*** The goblin takes the Disengage or Hide action.
