# Drider Priestess of Lloth (Boss)

*Large Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil*

**Armor Class** 19 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 123 (13d10 + 52)
**Speed** 30 ft., climb 30 ft.

| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT     | WIS     | CHA     |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- |
| 16 (+3) | 19 (+4) | 18 (+4) | 13 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) |

**Skills** Perception +6, Stealth +10
**Senses** darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 16
**Languages** Elvish, Undercommon
**Challenge** 6 (2,300 XP), **Proficiency Bonus** +3

### Traits

***Spider Climb.*** The priestess can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the priestess has Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls.

***Web Walker.*** The priestess ignores movement restrictions caused by webs, and she knows the location of any other creature in contact with the same web.

### Spellcasting

The priestess is a level 4 Cleric spellcaster. Wisdom is her spellcasting ability. Her spell save DC is 14, and she has +6 to hit with spell attacks. These numbers use the Drider's Wisdom 16 (+3) and Proficiency Bonus +3. She has the following Cleric spells prepared as prayers to Lloth:

- ***Cantrips, at will:***
  - *Guidance:* touch; no save or attack; choose one skill, and one willing creature adds 1d4 to ability checks using that skill; concentration, up to 1 minute.
  - *Sacred Flame:* 60 ft.; Dexterity save DC 14; 1d8 Radiant damage on a failed save, and the target gains no benefit from cover for the save; instantaneous; no concentration.
  - *Thaumaturgy:* 30 ft.; no save or attack; creates one minor supernatural effect such as a loud voice, trembling flame, or harmless omen; up to 1 minute; no concentration.
  - *Toll the Dead:* 60 ft.; Wisdom save DC 14; 1d8 Necrotic damage on a failed save, or 1d12 Necrotic if the target is missing any Hit Points; instantaneous; no concentration.
- ***1st level, 4 slots:***
  - *Bane:* 30 ft.; Charisma save DC 14 for up to three creatures; subtract 1d4 from attack rolls and saving throws on a failed save; concentration, up to 1 minute.
  - *Command:* 60 ft.; Wisdom save DC 14; one creature follows a one-word command on its next turn on a failed save; 1 round; no concentration.
  - *Guiding Bolt:* 120 ft. ranged spell attack at +6; 4d6 Radiant damage on a hit, and the next attack roll against the target before the end of the priestess's next turn has Advantage; 1 round; no concentration.
  - *Inflict Wounds:* touch; Constitution save DC 14; 2d10 Necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one; instantaneous; no concentration.
- ***2nd level, 3 slots:***
  - *Hold Person:* 60 ft.; Wisdom save DC 14 against one Humanoid; Paralyzed on a failed save, with a repeat save at the end of each of the target's turns; concentration, up to 1 minute.
  - *Silence:* 120 ft., 20 ft. radius sphere; no save; no sound can be created within or pass through the area, creatures fully inside it are Deafened, and spells with Verbal components cannot be cast there; concentration, up to 10 minutes.
  - *Spiritual Weapon:* 60 ft.; melee spell attack at +6 when cast and as a Bonus Action on later turns; 1d8 + 3 Force damage on a hit; concentration, up to 1 minute.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The priestess makes two attacks, using Foreleg or Three-Headed Snake Whip in any combination.

***Foreleg.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +7, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Piercing damage.

***Three-Headed Snake Whip.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +8, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d4 + 5) Slashing damage from a magic whip plus 3 (1d4 + 1) Poison damage. If this attack is a Critical Hit, the target must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or have the Poisoned condition. The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

### Bonus Actions

***Magic of the Spider Queen (Recharge 5-6).*** The priestess casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):

- *Darkness:* 60 ft.; no save or attack; creates a 15 ft. radius sphere of magical darkness; concentration, up to 10 minutes.
- *Faerie Fire:* 60 ft., 20 ft. cube; Dexterity save DC 14 or a target sheds dim light, cannot benefit from being Invisible, and attack rolls against it have Advantage; concentration, up to 1 minute.
- *Web:* 60 ft., 20 ft. cube; Dexterity save DC 14 or be Restrained by webs, with Strength checks to escape; the area is Difficult Terrain and lightly obscured; concentration, up to 1 hour.
