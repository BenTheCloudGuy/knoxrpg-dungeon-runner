# Large Snake (Constrictor Snake Chassis)

*Large Beast, Unaligned*

**Armor Class** 12
**Hit Points** 13 (2d10 + 2)
**Speed** 30 ft., swim 30 ft.

| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT    | WIS     | CHA    |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------ | ------- | ------ |
| 15 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 1 (-5) | 10 (+0) | 3 (-4) |

**Senses** blindsight 10 ft., passive Perception 10
**Languages** None
**Challenge** 1/4 (50 XP), **Proficiency Bonus** +2

### Actions

***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage.

***Constrict.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Grappled condition, escape DC 12. Until this grapple ends, the target has the Restrained condition, and the snake can't constrict another target.
