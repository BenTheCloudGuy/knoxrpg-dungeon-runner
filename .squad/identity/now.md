# Team Focus - Now

**Last updated:** 2026-09-06T16:51:49-04:00

## Current focus

Scrying Stone and crystal canon has shifted. The 7 Crystal Shards are now dungeon props for color-locked doors and the final exit-lock puzzle only. Real-world prizes are separate prize Item Cards from Treasure Goblins.

## Recent decisions

- Crystals no longer map to real-world prizes, treasure locations, bigger Treasure Items, prize tiers, or prize cards.
- The Black Crystal is one of the seven exit crystals and has no prize role or grand-prize gate rule.
- The Scrying Stone reveals clues for color-locked doors and the correct final exit-lock order, plus room and Xhal'theris context where useful.
- The first Scrying Stone remains in the Portal Room.
- Room loot is limited to consumables, mundane gear, and coin.
- Permanent magic items, including +1 weapons, wondrous items, and spellbooks, come only from Treasure Goblin vendors.
