# Team Wisdom

Hard-won lessons that apply across the squad. Append-only.

## Voice

- The DM test: would a real person say this out loud at the table? If not, rewrite.
- No em-dashes. No "not X, but Y." No flowery prose. Concrete nouns and verbs.
- Xhal'theris is theatrical but clinical — he announces, he taunts, he never explains the game in straight terms.

## Design

- This is a grinder. Pressure beats fairness. Death is a feature.
- Players need to *choose* between safety and reward. If a path is both safe and rewarding, it's broken.
- The best loot and prize cards sit behind real lethality. Never hand them out on the safe path.

## Workflow

- Read [README.md](../../README.md) and [prizes.md](../../prizes.md) before writing any new room or prop. They are canon.
- Reuse the existing room format ([ArtificersLair.md](../../rooms/ArtificersLair.md)) before inventing a new structure.
- When canon is unclear, ask the user. Don't invent.
