# Orchestration: Chilchuck — Vial of Poison respec

**Date:** 2026-09-04 16:00
**Prompt summary:** Respec the Treasure Goblin Vial of Poison mechanics to a coating-and-DoT model.
**Files touched:** items/magic-items/potions/vial-of-poison.md, .squad/agents/chilchuck/history.md, .squad/decisions/inbox/chilchuck-vial-of-poison.md
**Handoffs created:** .squad/decisions/inbox/chilchuck-vial-of-poison.md
**Outcome:** Chilchuck rewrote the Vial of Poison (Uncommon, 40 GP, injury). New rule: an action coats one weapon or up to three pieces of ammunition, one application per vial, coating lasts 1 minute. For that minute every creature that takes damage from the coated weapon takes an extra 2d4 Poison on the hit (no save on that part), then 1d4 Poison at the start of each of its turns until it succeeds on a DC 12 Constitution save rolled at the end of each of its turns; once a creature saves it can't be affected again (anti-stacking / anti-perma-DoT). Removed the old "first creature struck only" and "DC 12 or Poisoned condition" clauses. Kept DC 12 — the 2d4 on-hit is automatic and the save only ends the minor 1d4 trickle, keeping it in the standard injury-poison band and clear of the 2026-06-26 balance bans (finite consumable, player-side offense). Frontmatter unchanged. Coordinator re-rendered the card and rebuilt the deck PDF afterward.
