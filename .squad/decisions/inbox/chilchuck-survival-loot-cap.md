# Survival Loot Power Cap

**Date:** 2026-09-02 · **Owner:** Chilchuck · **Status:** OPEN (team review)

Loot placed in The Vault of the Starving Mind must aid survival without breaking the intended lethality. Party: 8-12 PCs, level 3, 40 HP cap, no long rests, 3 short rests, Mind Flayer host (Xhal'theris), gated crystal paths.

## Cap — what may be placed as loot
- +1 weapons, armor, and shields only. No +2/+3, no named legendaries.
- Healing is single-use potions only (`potion-of-healing`). No recurring or passive healing items.
- Spellcasting comes from consumable spell scrolls only. No recharging wands or staffs.
- Utility must be finite or bounded (`bag-of-holding`, `rope-of-climbing`, `driftglobe`, `feather-token-*`, resistance potions).
- The spellbook (`enduring-spellbook`) is allowed, treated as a package of scrolls.

## Banned as loot (per 2026-06-26 verdicts)
- Recurring/at-will healing, hard crowd control, flight, and teleport items.
- Save-or-die weapons (may be used against the party or on bosses, never handed to players).
- Anti-Mind-Flayer rings (`ring-of-mind-shielding`, `ring-of-free-action`) only as deliberate, telegraphed rewards, never random loot.
- Scrolls: `scroll-of-tarrasque-summoning`, `scroll-of-titan-summoning`, `nether-scroll-of-azumar`, `scroll-of-the-comet`, `scroll-of-spell-power`, `scroll-of-nightmares`.
