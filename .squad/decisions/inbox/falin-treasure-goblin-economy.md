# Decision: Treasure Goblin Vendor Gold Economy

**Date:** 2026-09-02 · **Owner:** Falin (Prize Steward) · **Status:** OPEN (team review)
**Requested by:** Ben

## Decision

Adopt a deflated, in-dungeon magic-item price scale (expressed as `cost: N GP` frontmatter, matching existing card format) sold by Treasure Goblin vendors, funded by a ~1,800 GP total dungeon treasure budget. Players spend found gold at the goblins to partially kit out without trivializing the lethality.

## Price scale (deflated, well below official 5e)

| Item | Vendor price |
| --- | --- |
| +1 weapon | 150 GP |
| +1 light armor | 100 GP |
| +1 medium armor | 150 GP |
| +1 heavy armor | 200 GP |
| +1 shield | 100 GP |
| Spell scroll - cantrip | 10 GP |
| Spell scroll - 1st | 25 GP |
| Spell scroll - 2nd | 50 GP |
| Spell scroll - 3rd | 100 GP |
| Potion of healing | 25 GP |
| Potion of greater healing | 75 GP |
| Resistance / utility potion | 40 GP |
| Bag of holding | 200 GP |
| Rope of climbing | 75 GP |
| Driftglobe | 100 GP |
| Feather token (feather fall) | 25 GP |

**Themed spellbook (5 scrolls):** sum the five scroll prices by level, then apply a 10% bundle discount, rounded to a clean number. Current six books land at 160-210 GP.

## Dungeon treasure budget

- **~1,800 GP total**, roughly 150 GP per player for a 12-PC party.
- Splits to about 900-1,050 GP of gear-tier value and 600-750 GP of consumables/utility, with a small buffer in high-danger optional zones.
- Enough for a partial kit-out (about one meaningful item per player), not a full one. Dead PCs' gold and gear feed the survivors.

## Consistency with canon

- `cost: N GP` frontmatter matches existing mundane-item cards, so Senshi can apply prices uniformly.
- The prize-token rate (1 GP = $0.10) applies only to out-of-game merchandise, not to in-dungeon Treasure Goblin barter.
- Tier ladder: mundane (PHB) -> magic items (deflated goblin prices) -> out-of-game prizes (USD conversion).
