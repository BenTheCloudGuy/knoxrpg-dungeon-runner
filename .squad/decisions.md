## 2026-10-02 — GM Guide Cleanup Round

### D-001 — Read-aloud secret-leak policy (Marcille)
Read-aloud boxes contain **only what the PCs can perceive at that moment**. No hidden trap outcomes, unseen ambushers, secret doors, or mechanics may leak into entering boxes. Trigger/reveal boxes (read after a thing is discovered or triggered) are allowed to describe the revealed element.
