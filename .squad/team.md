## Project Context

- **Project:** knoxrpg-dungeon-runner
- **Product:** The Vault of the Starving Mind — lethal D&D 5e (2024) one-shot grinder
- **Players:** 8–12, all level 3, capped at 40 HP
- **Layout:** ~5' x 7' Dwarven Forge dungeon, H-shaped with a lava/ruins divider between Caves and Stone Dungeon
- **Stack:** Markdown only — no code, no build

- **Prize System:** 7 crystals (ROYGBIV + Black grand prize, $150 gift card) read via the Scrying Stone prop
