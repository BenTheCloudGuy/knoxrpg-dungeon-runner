## Project Context

- **Project:** knoxrpg-dungeon-runner
- **Product:** The Vault of the Starving Mind — lethal D&D 5e (2024) one-shot grinder
- **Players:** 8–12, all level 3, capped at 40 HP
- **Layout:** 42 sq ft of Dwarven Forge terrain across two tables, H-shaped with a lava/ruins divider between Caves and Stone Dungeon
- **Stack:** Markdown only — no code, no build
- **Host:** Xhal'theris, the Velvet Maw (Mind Flayer announcer / tormentor)
- **Prize System:** Real-world prizes are prize Item Cards, found or bought from Treasure Goblins. Crystals are separate exit-lock puzzle props (7 Shards, star-lock order), read via the Scrying Stone.
- **Owner:** Ben Mitchell (BenTheCloudGuy)
- **Created:** 2026-05-30
